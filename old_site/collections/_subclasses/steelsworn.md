***They say that a weapon is as much a person as the one who wields it, that it feels, thinks, and has a personality. Many form a special relationship with their weapon, but they still see it as merely a tool to deal death. A select few wield their weapon knowing that it has a soul and must be treated as such. These Steelsworn Fighters often manifest their bonds with weapons through deep emotional connections or earn their respect in a trial of combat. Through this bond, their weapon grants them arcane abilities that make them a terrifying force on the battlefield.***

| Fighter Level | Features |
|----------------|--------|
| 3rd            | Bonded Weapon, Enhancements, Minor Arcana |
| 7th            | Hastened Arcana |
| 10th           | Enhancements |
| 11th           | Sunder Will |

### Bonded Weapon
At 3rd level, you learn a ritual that creates a magical bond between yourself and one weapon. You perform the ritual over the course of 1 hour, which can be done during a short rest. The weapon must be within your reach throughout the ritual, at the conclusion of which you touch the weapon and forge the bond. A bonded weapon forms a nascent sentience that grows as you get stronger.
<div class="break"> </div>
Once you have bonded a weapon to yourself, you can't be disarmed of that weapon unless you are incapacitated. If it is on the same plane of existence, you can summon that weapon as a bonus action on your turn, causing it to teleport instantly to your hand. Your bonded weapon must be on your person in order to use the abilities gained from this subclass.

### Enhancements
At 3rd level, the bond between you and your weapon grants magically influences your abilities.  
<div class="break"> </div>
**Enhancements.** You learn three [Enhancements](#enhancements-list) of your choice. Many Enhancements improve an attack in some way, though you must be holding your Bonded Weapon to use them. You can use only one Enhancement per attack. You learn two additional Enhancements of your choice at 7th, 10th, and 15th level. Each time you gain a level in this class , you can also replace one Enhancement you know with a different one.

**Bond Point.** You have four Bond Points which are used to fuel your Enhancements. You regain all of your expended Bond Points when you finish a short or long rest. You gain another Bond Points at 7th level and one more at 11th level.

**Saving Throws.** Some of your Enhancements require your target to make a saving throw to resist the Enhancement’s effects. The saving throw DC is calculated as follows:

**Enhancement save DC** = 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Intelligence modifier.

### Minor Arcana
Also at 3rd level, you gain two cantrips from the wizard spell list. You use Intelligence as your spell casting modifier for these spells. You must be holding your Bonded Weapon to cast these cantrips.
<div class="break"> </div>

You learn an additional cantrip at 7th and 15th level.

### Hastened Magic
At 7th level, when you use an Enhancement on your turn, you may cast a cantrip in place of a weapon attack.

### Sunder Will
At 10th level, your weapon now psychically affects creatures that are struck by it. When you succeed an attack against a creature using your Bonded Weapon, they have disadvantage on their next saving throw against your spells or Enhancements. This may only be used once per round.

### Weapon of Alert
At 15th level, your weapon stands at the ready, even if you are not. If you are surprised or become unconscious, your bonded weapon immediately rolls its own initiative and acts on its turn until you pick it up again as a bonus action.
<div class="break"> </div>

While acting on its own, the weapon uses your game statistics and can take one of the following actions on its turn: Attack (two weapon attacks), Dash, Help, or Shove. When it uses the Help action, it can rouse you from unconsciousness if you are asleep. The weapon can move up to 30 ft. on its turn, but it can not enter a space that is farther than 30 ft. away from you. If the weapon is ever more than 30 ft. away, it falls to the ground and becomes inert.
<div class="break"> </div>

The weapon can use your Enhancements, but it does not benefit from or use any of your other class features while acting on its own.

### Perfect Bond
At 18th level, you can fully connect your mind to the budding sentience of your weapon as an action. For 1 minute, when you roll a weapon attack using your Bonded Weapon, you treat any roll that is 8 or below as a 9. This feature can only be used once per long rest.

## Enhancements

### Flash Step
When you hit a creature with your Bonded Weapon, you may immediately teleport up to 20 ft. away in a flash of light. All creatures within 5 ft. of the point you appear in must succeed a Constitution saving throw or be Blinded until the end of their next turn.

### Roaring Flame
When you hit a creature with your Bonded Weapon, you may cause a plume of fire to emanate from your weapon. All creatures within 10 ft. of your target, excluding yourself, take a d6 of fire damage.

### Light Footed
As a bonus action, take the Dash action. Also, you gain a Flying speed equal to half of your movement.

### Trickery
As a bonus action while holding your Bonded Weapon, send an illusory mirage towards a creature within 20 ft., prompting it to make an Intelligence saving throw. On a fail, they are forced to defend themselves from a false enemy. The illusion has an AC of 10 + your Dexterity modifier and has 1 hit point. Until the end of its next turn, they are unable to leave the illusion's melee range unless they Disengage or can otherwise avoid Opportunity Attacks. Also, other creatures attacking the target can benefit from features that require an ally within 5 ft. of the enemy.

### Rising Force
When you hit a creature with your Bonded Weapon, force the creature to make a Strength saving throw. On a fail, launch both of you up to 20 ft. into the air and begin floating until the end of your next turn. Unless the creature has a Flying speed, it is considered Restrained for the duration of the effect. Unless you have a flying speed, your movement is reduced to 0.

### Descending Strike
As a weapon attack with your Bonded Weapon, fall straight down to the ground and smite a creature from above. Make an attack against a creature that is at equal elevation or below you and is horizontally within your weapon's reach, regardless. On a hit, they take an additional d4 of damage for every 10 ft. you fall and they must make a Strength saving throw. On a fail, they are knocked Prone.

### Delayed Blast
When you hit a creature with your Bonded Weapon, force the creature to make a Constitution saving throw. On a fail, they are Restrained until the end of your turn. When the condition ends, they are pushed 15 ft. away and take 1d8 fire damage.

### Devil's Aura
Your Bonded Weapon subtly influences the perception other creatures have of you. Gain advantage on a Charisma check. This must be done before knowing the outcome of the roll.

### Thought Stealer
Your Bonded Weapon can softly probe the minds of others around you, granting you greater insight into their psyche. Cast the Detect Thoughts spell as an action, using your Intelligence as the spellcasting modifier.

### Arcane Attunement
Your Bonded Weapon reacts slightly to magic in the air. Cast the Detect Magic spell as an action, using your Intelligence as the spellcasting modifier.

### Gale Wind
Your Bonded Weapon unleashes torrents of air to beat back your foes. As an action, cast the Gust of Wind spell, using your Intelligence as the spellcasting modifier.

### Demonic Vengeance
Your Bonded Weapon retaliates harshly to those who would do you harm. You may cast the Hellish Rebuke spell, using your Intelligence as the spellcasting modifier.

### You Shall Die
*Prerequisite: 7th level*

<div class="break"></div>

As an action, strike out at those who would stand in the way of your search for power. Force all creatures in a 20 ft. radius around you to make a Dexterity saving throw. On a fail, you strike them with your Bonded Weapon and they take an additional 1d8 force damage.

### Umbra's Sight
*Prerequisite: 10th level*

<div class="break"></div>

Your Bonded Weapon allows you to see past fabricated realities. As an action, gain True Sight up to 20 ft for 1 minute. You also become immune to Charms and being Frightened through magical means for 1 minute.

### Dimension Slash
*Prerequisite: 15th level*

<div class="break"></div>

Your Bonded Weapon can cut through space to open portals to other places. Cast the Teleport spell as an action. This can only be used once per long rest.
