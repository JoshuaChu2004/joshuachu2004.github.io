To overcome the fact that normal animals and pets do not get stronger as your character does, Berry Rules makes sure that pets that serve no purpose in combat will not be targeted by any attack (even AOE spells like Fireball). This does not protect animals that scale with your level, familiars, summons, or anything similar. If you have a pet do anything that would put them at risk, they can now be targeted by hostile creatures.
