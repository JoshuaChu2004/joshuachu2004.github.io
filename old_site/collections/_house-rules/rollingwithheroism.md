When attempting an ability check, saving throw, or attack roll, a character may declare a Roll with Heroism, and then describe how they break the bounds of normalcy and step into the extraordinary. A character may describe how they strike out at a creatures eyes to blind it, inspire a group of enemies to join their side, or scale up to the back of a raging dragon. If the DM accepts their description, they may then Roll with Heroism, with a DC higher than normal. On a success, the character gains more than what a normal success would elicit and are also awarded with a Heroic Inspiration. On a failure, the character suffers a consequence beyond what a normal failure would elicit.

If a character attempts to perform an action that is particularly cinematic, difficult, and interesting, the DM can make the check a Roll with Heroism. A Roll with Heroism made in this way allows the character to use abilities that allow them to reroll the die, except for Heroic Inspiration.
