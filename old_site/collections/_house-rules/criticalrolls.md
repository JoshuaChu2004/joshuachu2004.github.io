A Natural (meaning just the value shown on the dice) 20 or a Natural 1 are Critical Successes and Critical Failures respectively. When landing a critical success on an attack, you roll the damage of the attack as normal, then double it at the end. You do the same for any additional damage the attack may do, such as Divine Smite or Booming Blade. A critical failure on an attack will always miss.

However, landing a NAT 20 or NAT 1 outside of attacking rolls usually means you perform either the best possible (and perhaps slightly better) outcome or the worst possible outcome respectively.

**Examples:**
- Even if you roll a NAT 20 trying to fly and jump off a cliff, you won't fly. You'll be carried by a freak wind and land with minor scratches.
- Even if you roll a NAT 1 trying to pet a dog, the worst that will happen is you step on its tail.
