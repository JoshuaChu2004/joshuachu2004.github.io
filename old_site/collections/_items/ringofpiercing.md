A large ring made for a person much bigger than normal. For a medium or small creature, this is more like a bracelet. The gemstone is a large, thin ruby with very sharp points. The ring has 3 charges. When you make an attack or cast a spell that does piercing damage while wearing this ring, you can expend 1 of its charges to reroll up to 3 dice and must use the new rolls. The ring regains 1d3 charges at the end of a long rest.
