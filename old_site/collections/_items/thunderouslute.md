A magical lute whose strings were struck by lightning on an ever stormy landmass to the west. Or so the tale goes anyway. Whenever anyone attempts to play the lute, they would feel very minor shocks whenever they touched the strings. Not strong enough to waver the concentration of a trained musician but enough to be noticeable. The Thunderous Lute has three charges, which reset after a long rest.

<div class="break"></div>

**Shock Field.** Consuming one of the charges, the attuned user can cause a field of electricity to surround a single creature. The field will automatically morph so that the target is not harmed by the field. When any other creature makes contact with the field (touch spell, melee attack, etc.), they must make a Constitution Save with DC equal to the user's Spell Save DC. On a fail, the creature takes 2d4 lightning damage. If rolled a NAT 1, the creature is paralyzed for 1 round.

<div class="break"></div>

**Call to the Heavens.** Consuming two of the charges, the attuned user can summon down lightning from above as an action, striking a single creature. The creature must make a Dexterity Save with DC 14. On a fail, the creature takes 4d8 lightning damage and is stunned until the start of their next turn or half as much damage on a successful save.
