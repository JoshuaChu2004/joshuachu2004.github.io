An ethereal blue liquid swirls within the glass and small wisps of lightning, fire, and other magical effects can be seen. This concentrated liquid of pure source that Galaerd has concocted produces powerful, but inconsistent effects.

<div class="break"></div>

For 1 minute after drinking, roll on the Potency table to determine your effect.

<div class="break"></div>

**Potency Table (1d6):**

| d6 | Effect |
|-----|--------|
|1| You gain a +1 bonus to your saving throws.|
|2| Your speed is increased by 10 ft.|
|3| An illusory copy appears and begins mimicking your movements, granting you the effects of Mirror Image with 1 copy.|
|4| You gain 3 temporary hit points at the start of your turn.|
|5| The air around you begins to heat up and burn, dealing 1d6 fire damage to every creature that starts its turn or enters within 10 ft. of you.|
|6| You can reroll up to 3 damage die when you cast a damaging spell or make an attack.|
