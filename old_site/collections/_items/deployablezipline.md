A large metal tube with a trigger. It was originally meant for a special task force in Averon, though the project was canceled after it was deemed a failure. Upon pulling the trigger, a large arrow-like bolt attached to a metal rope shoots out the front and impales into practically anything within 150 ft. After being connected, the rope will naturally make itself taut and plant a spike into the ground below where it was shot. The tube will then eject eight handheld grips that attach and accelerate the user along the rope, even vertically upwards.

<div class="break"></div>

The zipline can be used as a weapon and any creature impaled is restricted in movement (cannot move out of the rope range until the spike is taken out or rope is cut).
