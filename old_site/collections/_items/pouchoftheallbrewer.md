You can pull a random potion out of your bag and choose to either drink it or toss it to another creature. Said creature can then drink the vial as a reaction. The vial will disappear after 5 minutes. Upon further investigation, it is impossible to determine the potions.

<div class="break"></div>

As a bonus action, pull out a random potion from your bag and roll a 1d6 to determine what type of potion it is.

<div class="break"></div>

**Potion Table (1d6):**

| d6 | Potion | Effect |
|-----|--------|--------|
|1| **Dud potion.** | No effect.|
|2| **Potion of Guidance.** | Gain a 1d4 to the creature's next roll.|
|3| **Potion of the Wind.** | Gain +10 movement speed and +1 to Dexterity Modifier until the end of their next turn.|
|4| **Potion of Healing.** | Gain 3d4 + 3 Hit Points.|
|5| **Potion of Weakness.** | Gain Improved Critical until the end of their next turn.|
|6| **Potion of Inspiration.** | Gain Inspiration.|
