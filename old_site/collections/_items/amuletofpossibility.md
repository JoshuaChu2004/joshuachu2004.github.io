A necklace with a cracked piece of glass as its gemstone. When you look within, you can see glimpses of different possibilities. This amulet has 3 charges. When a creature makes an ability check, attack roll, damage roll, or saving throw, you may expend a charge to reduce or increase the roll by a d8 (to a minimum of 1 and a maximum equal to the roll’s highest possible value) as a reaction. You may use this after knowing the outcome of the roll. The amulet regains 1 charge at dawn.
