A pendant shaped like a rose, the holy symbol of the god of love. It is said to create an air of love and calm around the one wearing it. Once per long rest, when a creature you can see makes a WIS Save or Insight check against a charm or sniffs out lies/exterior motives, you may use up a charge and impose disadvantage on that roll. You may also use a charge to give yourself advantage on a persuasion or deception check. A charge can only be used before you know the roll's outcome.
