A very unique design for a weapon. A small snake is seen coiling around the handle that acts as a grip. It is not magical, but actually uses clever contraptions that keeps the edge of the blade tipped with poison. A small hole at the bottom of its handle can bold a 1 oz bottle of any liquid. The liquid must have some effect when injected or ingested in order for it to work with this weapon. Whenever landing a hit, the target must make a CON save with DC determined by the liquid in order for it to have its intended effect. 

<div class="break"></div>

For each 1 oz. bottle of liquid, the weapon has 5 uses before it uses up all the liquid, and then a new bottle must be placed in.

<div class="break"></div>

This weapon is very well made, granting it bonuses equal to a +1 weapon.
