Shatter the resolve of your enemies with a long-range vertical slash. All creatures within a 20 ft. line must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a fail, they take 1d10 damage of your weapon's type and have disadvantage on their next attack roll, ability check, or saving throw.

<div class="break"></div>

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, the range of the line increases by 5 ft.
