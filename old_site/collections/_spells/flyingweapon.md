title: Flying Weapon
school: Transmutation
level: 4th
casting_time: 1 action
range: Self (30 ft.)

components: V, S, M (a weapon)
ritual: false
concentration: true

 - list: Armswright

You cause a weapon you are holding to float into the air and fly toward a point within range. When the weapon arrives, you may immediately make one weapon attack against a creature within its reach.

<div class="break"></div>

While the spell persists, the weapon remains animated. At the start of each of your turns, you can move the weapon up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space you can see. On your turn, you can use the Attack action to make an attack with the flying weapon as if you were wielding it, using your normal ability modifier and proficiency bonus.

<div class="break"></div>

If you lose concentration, it falls harmlessly to the ground.
