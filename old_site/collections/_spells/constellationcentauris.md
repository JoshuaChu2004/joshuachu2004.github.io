Rapid strikes all around you. Make a melee weapon attack against two creatures within 30 ft. of you. On a hit, your weapon deals cosmic damage instead of its normal damage type and they are pushed 5 ft. away from you.

<div class="break"></div>

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, you may target an additional creature for each slot level above 1st.
