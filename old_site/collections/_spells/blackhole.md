You summon a small black hole into existence somewhere within range, where it begins to suck in all objects and creatures. When a creature starts its turn or enters within 60 ft. of the black hole, it must make a Strength saving throw. Upon fail, they are pulled up to 20 ft. closer to the black hole. Multiple creatures can occupy the same space as the black hole, no matter their size category. Creatures that occupy the same space as the black hole become Restrained as they are sucked in and take 5d10 cosmic damage at the start of their turn. A trapped creature can escape by succeeding in the Strength saving throw and leaving the space. When the spell ends, all creatures and objects are shot back out of the black hole, and it disappears.

**Star Charged.** The area within 60 ft. of the black hole is now considered difficult terrain unless the creature moves towards the blackhole.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 9th level or higher, the cosmic damage increases by 1d10, for each slot level above 8th.
