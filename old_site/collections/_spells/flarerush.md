Holding your weapon to the side, your body releases hot sparks before your entire body becomes engulfed in a ball of flames. You dash in any direction up to 30 ft. while slashing your weapon, passing through any creatures along your path. Every creature within 10 ft. of your path must make a Dexterity save. Upon fail, they take 4d8 fire damage, and half as much on a success. Creatures in the path that fail the saving throw are also hit by your weapon, taking additional damage.
<div class="break"></div>

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 4th level or higher, the damage increases by 1d8 for each slot level above 3rd.
