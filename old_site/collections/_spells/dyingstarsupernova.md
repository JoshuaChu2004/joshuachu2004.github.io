Mimick the death of a star and explode outwards, striking out all around you. Make a melee weapon attack against each creature within 10 ft. of you. On a hit, they must make a Constitution saving throw. On a fail, they take an additional 1d8 cosmic damage and are pushed 10 ft. away.

<div class="break"></div>

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, the sphere radius increases by 5 ft. for each slot level above 1st.
