Call down a beam of cosmic radiation to strike anyone caught in its ray. Every creature within the beam must make a Constitution saving throw. Upon fail, they take 1d8 cosmic damage.

**Star Charged.** The beam now amplifies any allies that are in its radius rather than hurting them. Their next attack deals an additional d6 of cosmic damage.

This spell's damage increases by 1d8 when you reach 5th level (2d8), 11th level (3d8), and 17th level (4d8).
