As a reaction to failing a Dexterity saving throw or a creature making an attack roll against you, you slow down your perception of time, allowing you to dodge more effectively. You have advantage on Dexterity saving throws, creatures have disadvantage on attack rolls, Grapple checks, and Shove checks against you until the start of your next turn.
