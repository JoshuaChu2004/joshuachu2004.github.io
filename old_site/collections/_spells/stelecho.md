Strike solid stone or metal with a weapon, causing thunderous reverberations to emanate outwards. The vibration briefly outlines a 30 ft. sphere around you. Within the area, you automatically detect:

- Hidden passages, false panels, and secret doors.
- Traps and hollow spaces.
- Creatures, even if they are invisible or heavily obscured.

The spell passes through cover but does not penetrate magical barriers, such as a wall of force, nor can it penetrate more than an inch of lead.
