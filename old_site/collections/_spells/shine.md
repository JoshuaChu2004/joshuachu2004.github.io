You body begins to glow slightly until you suddenly release a bright flash of light. All creatures that rely on sight within 30 ft. of you must make a Constitution saving throw. Upon fail, they are blinded until the end of the spell. They may reroll the Constitution saving throw at the end of their turn.

**Star Charged.** As an action, you may release an additional flash, forcing creatures to make the same Constitution saving throw, given that they are not already blind.
