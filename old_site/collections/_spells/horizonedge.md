Cut through in a horizontal slash to crash down on any defense. Make a melee weapon attack against a creature within range. On a hit, they take an additional 1d8 cosmic damage and the next attack against them has advantage.

<div class="break"></div>

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, the damage increases by 1d8 for each slot level above 1st.
