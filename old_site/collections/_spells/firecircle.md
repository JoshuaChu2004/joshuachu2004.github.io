Channeling fire into your weapon, you sweep it in a circle around you. All creatures within 15 ft. must make a Dexterity saving throw. Upon fail, they take 3d6 fire damage and half as much on a success. Creatures within 5 ft. that fail the saving throw are also hit by your weapon, taking additional damage.
<div class="break"></div>

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, the damage increases by 1d6 for each slot level above 2nd.
