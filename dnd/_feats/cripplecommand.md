title: Cripple Command
content_type: Feat

Every group has a leader, a voice of authority. No matter the race, age, or even species, all groups have someone at the helm. Once they are taken out, the group falls into chaos. You have learned to single out such leaders and deal with them in order to strike fear and chaos in their subordinates.
- Increase your Charisma ability score by 1, to a maximum of 20
- At the start of combat, you can determine the leader of the group that you are fighting. The leader can be the group's strategist (e.x. general), symbolic figure (e.x. flagbearer), or simply the strongest creature.
- Whenever you make an attack that brings the leader under 0 Hit Points, you may defeat them in a way that strikes fear in the heart of all other enemies. All hostile creatures must make a Wisdom saving throw with DC equal to 8 + your Intimidation modifier. Upon fail, they are frightened for 1 minute. They may remake this saving throw at the end of their turn.
