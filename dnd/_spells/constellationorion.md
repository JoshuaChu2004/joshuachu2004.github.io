Conjure the essence of the legendary hunter and prepare your blade. When you make an attack roll, you may choose to add a +3 bonus to the To Hit roll. This can be done after rolling but before knowing the result. If you hit on the attack, the attack deals an additional 1d8 cosmic damage.

<div class="break"></div>

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, you may add a +3 bonus on an additional attack for each slot level above 1st.
