You launch a celestial tether to a target creature. Make a melee spell attack against a creature within range. Upon hit, they are grappled and take 2d8 cosmic damage. They remain grappled unless you leave their melee range or they succeed a Strength saving throw at the end of their turn. At the start of every round that they are grappled, they take an additional 1d8 cosmic damage.

**Star Charged.** Instead of being grappled, the creature is Restrained and you may leave their melee range without ending the spell.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, the damage increases by 1d8, for each slot level above 1st.
