As a reaction to a creature hitting you with an attack, evade while attempting to limit the vision of your opponent. The creature must make a Constitution saving throw. On a fail, their attack must reroll the attack's To Hit roll, taking the lower result, and they are Blinded until the end of your next turn. On a success, only reroll the To Hit roll.
