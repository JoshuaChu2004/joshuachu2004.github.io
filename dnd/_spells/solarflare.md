Your skin begins to glow as you suddenly expel celestial energy around you. Any creatures within 20 ft. must make a Dexterity saving throw. Upon fail, they take 5d8 cosmic damage. The blast goes around corners. Dim light lingers in the area for 1 minute after the spell is cast. If you cast this spell in complete darkness, affected creatures take an additional 2d8 radiant damage, half on a success.

**Star Charged.** Affected creatures are set alight with cosmic energy. They take 1d8 cosmic damage at the start of your turn until they use an action to put themselves out.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 4th level or higher, the damage increases by 1d8, for each slot level above 3rd.
