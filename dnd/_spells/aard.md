Expel a telekinetic blast that sends creatures and objects away. All creatures within a 15 ft. cube with the middle of an edge centered on you must make a Strength saving throw. On a failed save, creatures are pushed 10 ft. away from you and knocked prone. On a successful one, creatures are pushed 5 ft. away. Unsecured objects are also pushed 10 ft.
<div class="break"></div>
This sign’s push distance increases by 5 ft. when you reach 5th level (15 ft.), 10th level (20 ft.), and 15th level (25 ft.). This does not affect the distance pushed on a successful save.
