Tap into an essence of fiendish persuasion, granting you the silver tongue of a devil. When making a Charisma check, you may treat any roll 6 or below as a 7.
<div class="break"></div>

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, you may treat any roll 6 or below as a 7 for each slot level above 2nd.
