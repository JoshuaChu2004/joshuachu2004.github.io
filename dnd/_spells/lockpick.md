Magically open a simple and mundane lock with the touch of your fingers. The lock can not be magical and can not be very sophisticated.

**Star Charged.** The spell can now unlock advanced and complicated locks, even magical ones. However, the user must succeed a spellcasting check with DC determined by the DM.
