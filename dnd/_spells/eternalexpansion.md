Channel your forward momentum to devastate creatures that are moved by your abilities. Whenever a creature is moved or pushed by you, they take 1d6 cosmic damage.

<div class="break"></div>

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, the damage increases by 1d6 for each slot level above 1st.
