title: Fiendish Charm
school: Enchantment
level: 4th
casting_time: 1 action
range: 30 ft.
duration: 1 hour (concentration)
components: V, S, M (a ring with a ruby gem, worth at least 25 gp, which the spell consumes)
ritual: false
concentration: true
spell_lists:
 - list: Fiendblade
finished: true
---
Captivate the mind of a creature within 30 ft. that you can see through your infernal inheritance. The creature must succeed a Wisdom saving throw or be magically charmed for 1 day. The charmed target obeys any and all spoken commands, except directly suicidal ones. If the target takes damage from you, other creatures, or you give it a suicidal command, the target may repeat the saving throw, ending the effect on a success. When a creature succeeds the saving throw, they are immune to this spell for 24 hours.
