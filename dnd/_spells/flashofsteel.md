Channel a creature within range with temporal energy, allowing them to adjust their speed with their strikes. The creature's melee attacks deal an additional d6 of damage.
<div class="break"></div>

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, you may target an additional creature for every 2 slot levels above 2nd.
