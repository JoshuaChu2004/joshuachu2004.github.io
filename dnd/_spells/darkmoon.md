Perform a swift horizontal slash in a crescent shape before sheathing your weapon. Make a melee weapon attack against a creature within range. On a hit, they take an additional 1d8 radiant damage. Until the start of your next turn, if a creature enters within 5 ft. of you or misses a melee attack against you, you may use your reaction to make an attack against that creature.

<div class="break"></div>

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, the damage increases by 1d8 for each slot level above 1st.
