You summon the power of stars within yourself and begin to build up enough energy in order to go supernova. Upon exploding, all creatures within a 30 ft. radius take 12d10 cosmic damage. While other creatures within a 60 ft. radius must make a Dexterity saving throw. Upon fail, they take 10d8 cosmic damage or half as much on a success.

**Star Charged.** Truly be a blaze of glory in the sky. The radius for both thresholds is doubled, but you also take half the point-blank damage dealt.
