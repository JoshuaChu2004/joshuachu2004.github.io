Calming light begins to wash over you and any creature within 30 ft. of you. As an action, choose a creature within the radius to make a Charisma saving throw. Upon fail, their emotions are quelled until the end of the spell. After the spell ends, all creatures will know that their emotions were affected. Creatures immune to being charmed are not affected by this spell.

**Star Charged.** You are able to cause the opposite affect, turning violent creatures to combat and calm creatures to hostility.
