title: Gravity Well

You magnify the gravity around you to be twice the normal amount. A radius of 15 ft. around you is considered difficult terrain. Every creature that enters or starts its turn in this space must make a Strength saving throw. Upon fail, they take 1d8 bludgeoning damage and fall Prone.

**Star Charged.** The radius of the well is increased to 25 ft. and creatures that fail the saving throw are pulled 5 ft. towards the center.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, the damage increases by 1d8, for each slot level above 1st.
