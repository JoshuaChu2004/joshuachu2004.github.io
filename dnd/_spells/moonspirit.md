Lash out with multiple slashes layered over each other, creating a vortex of steel. All creatures within a 20 ft. cone must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a fail, they take 1d10 damage of your weapon's type and 1d6 radiant damage, and half as much on a success.

<div class="break"></div>

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, the radiant damage increases by 1d6 for each slot level above 1st.
