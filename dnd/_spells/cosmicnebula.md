Dazzle and distract a creature with rapid and unpredictable movements. Make an attack against a creature within 20 ft. On a hit, they have disadvantage on their next attack and their speed is halved until the end of your next turn.

<div class="break"></div>

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, you may target an additional creature for each slot level above 1st.
