Target an area within range and severely disrupt the time stream for all creatures within a 20 ft. sphere. All creatures must make a Wisdom saving throw. On a fail, they take 3d8 psychic damage and must roll for an effect on the Desynchronization table. On a success they only take half damage.
<div class="break"></div>

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 4th level or higher, the damage increases by 1d8 and creatures rolling on the Desynchronization table have a +1 modifier to the roll for each slot level above 3rd.
