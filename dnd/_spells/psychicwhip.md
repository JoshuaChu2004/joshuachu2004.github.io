Conjure a metaphysical whip or other harmful instrument that leaves painful lashes on a creature's psyche. As an action, force a creature within 30 ft. to make a Intelligence saving throw. Upon fail, they take 3d10 psychic damage and are Dazed until the end of your next turn. Upon success, they take half damage and are not dazed.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, the initial damage increases by 1d10 for each slot level above 3rd.
