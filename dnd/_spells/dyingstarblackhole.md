With hooks and grabs, pull your enemies within striking distance. Force every creature within 20 ft. to make a Strength saving throw. On a fail, they are pulled 10 ft. towards you.

<div class="break"></div>

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, the radius increases by 5 ft. for each slot level above 1st.
