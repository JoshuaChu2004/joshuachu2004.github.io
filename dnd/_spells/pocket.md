You can take any object lighter than 10 lbs. and place it into a pocket of space somewhere within the universe. You are only able to store one object at a time or else you will lose contact with the previous object. Only you can access this object again via casting this spell.

**Star Charged.** The capacity for the object grows to up to 200 lbs. and you can make a spell attack against a creature within Touch range to stuff them into the pocket, which operates similarly to being placed into a Bag of Holding. At the end of its turn, a creature may make a Constitution saving throw to be released from the Pocket.
