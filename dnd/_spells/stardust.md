You fill a sphere with a radius of 30 ft. around you with the dust of stars, lightly obscuring all creatures inside. All allied creatures that enter the sphere gain the effects of Light of the Cosmos, including your Constellation effect if it is active.

**Star Charged.** The sphere of Stardust now follows you and all hostile creatures must make a Wisdom saving throw or be Blinded.
