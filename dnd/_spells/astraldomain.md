Bring the domain of your birthright to your physical location, replacing the sky within a 100 ft. sphere with the image of the endless cosmos. While in this domain, you gain a fly speed equal to double your walking speed and any spell or attack that deals cosmic damage deals an additional d6. As a reaction to a creature making an attack or casting a damaging spell, you can change the damage type to cosmic damage.

**Star Charged.** Creatures can no longer leave the Domain until it is dispelled except through means of teleportation.
