An elusive, cunning strike, made without notice toward the enemy. Your next melee weapon attack has advantage and deals an additional 1d8 radiant damage.

<div class="break"></div>

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, the damage increases by 1d8 for each slot level above 1st.
