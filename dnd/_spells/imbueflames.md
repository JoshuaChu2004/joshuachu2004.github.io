Running your hand along a weapon you are holding, you magically instill it with flames. Until the spell ends, every melee attack made with the weapon deals an additional 1d8 fire damage.
<div class="break"></div>

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, the damage increases by 1d8 for each slot level above 1st.
