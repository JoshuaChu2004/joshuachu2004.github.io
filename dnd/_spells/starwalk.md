Allow you and your compatriots to break free of the planet's gravitational hold and walk amongst the stars. Up to six creatures of your choosing gain a fly speed equal to their walking speed and gain advantage on Saving Throws that would cause them to fall prone or be restrained.

**Star Charged.** Affected creatures no longer need oxygen to breath and their Fly speed is doubled for the duration.
