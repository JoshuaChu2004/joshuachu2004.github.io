Call down a small celestial body to strike a creature within range. Make a spell attack against the creature. On hit, they take 3d10 bludgeoning damage and 3d10 cosmic damage and is knocked prone.

**Star Charged.** The comet now creates a slight explosion within 10 ft. of its impact, forcing all creatures in the radius to make a Dexterity Saving Throw. Upon fail, they take the cosmic damage and half as much on a success.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 5th level or higher, the cosmic damage increases by 1d10, for each slot level above 4th.
