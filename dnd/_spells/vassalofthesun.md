With intense focus, your blood flares up as you begin to give off an intense heat from your body. All creatures that start their turn or enter within 20 ft. must make a Constitution saving throw. Upon a fail, they take 4d6 fire damage and half as much on a success.
<div class="break"></div>

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 5th level or higher, the damage increases by 1d6 for each slot level above 4th.
