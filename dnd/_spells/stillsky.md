Calm your mind and still your body. Increase your AC by +2 until the start of your next turn. Each time a creature misses an attack against you while this stance is active, they take 1d8 cosmic damage as you lash out against them.

<div class="break"></div>

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, the damage increases by 1d8 for each slot level above 1st.
