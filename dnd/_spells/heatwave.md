By slashing your weapon and calling forth the fire from within, you emanate a quick wave of fire in any direction that travels out in a 20 ft. line. All creatures caught within the line must make a Dexterity saving throw. On fail, they take 1d6 fire damage and half as much on a success. Any object that is not being worn or carried is set on fire.
<div class="break"></div>
This spell's damage increases by 1d6 when you reach 5th level (2d6), 11th level (3d6), and 17th level (4d6).
