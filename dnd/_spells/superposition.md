You place down a glowing sphere where you are standing, and another appears anywhere within range. As a bonus action or a reaction to being hit, you are able to teleport to the other point if you are standing within 15 ft. of a sphere. The spheres have 1 HP and 10 AC, and if one is destroyed, the spell ends.

**Star Charged.** Your allies are able to use the spheres as well.
