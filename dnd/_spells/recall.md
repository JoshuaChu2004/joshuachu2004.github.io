Place a golden orb to act as a checkpoint in your individual timestream when you cast this spell. Before the spell ends, you may use your action to return to that point. You are brought back to where you were standing upon casting the spell and your Hit Points are returned to the amount you had upon casting the spell. If the golden orb is removed from its location or damaged during this spell, the spell automatically ends.
