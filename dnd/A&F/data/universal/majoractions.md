**Attack.** The most common action taken in combat, whether by swinging a sword, firing a bow, or brawling with your fists. See the attacks section for your attacks.
**Grappling.** A special unarmed strike against a creature no larger than one size larger than you. On a hit, you subject them to the Grappled and Exposed condition. 
**Shove.** A special unarmed strike against a creature no larger than one size larger than you. On a hit, you may push them back 5 ft. or knock them Prone. They are also subjected to the Exposed condition. 
**Cast a Spell.** Cast a spell that has a casting time of 1 action. 
**Dash.** When you take the Dash action, you increase your movement for the current turn by your current speed. 
**Dodge.** Attack rolls made against you have disadvantage if you can see the attacker, and you make Reflex saving throws with advantage until the start of your next turn. You lose this benefit if you are incapacitated or your speed drops to 0. 
**Help.** Aid another creature in a task or action. Until the start of your next turn, the creature you aid gains advantage on its next ability check or attack roll, so long as it is possible to help them in their task from where you currently are. Alternatively, collectively spend 2 Heroic Inspiration with an ally to take a Tag Team action.
**Hide.** Make a Dexterity (Stealth) check to attempt to hide from other creatures. The DM may set a DC or otherwise use the Passive Perception of the enemy creatures in the encounter.
**Ready.** Declare an action you will take and what condition(s) must be met before you take that action as a reaction. 
**Search.** Make an appropriate ability check to find something.
