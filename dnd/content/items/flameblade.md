# Flameblade

*Weapon (longsword), rare (requires attunement)*

This elegant longsword is forged from a mysterious red metal that seems to glow with inner fire. When drawn, wisps of flame dance along its blade.

## Properties

- **Damage:** 1d8 slashing + 1d6 fire
- **Damage Type:** Slashing and Fire
- **Properties:** Versatile (1d10)
- **Weight:** 3 lbs.

## Magical Properties

### Fiery Strike
When you hit with an attack using this weapon, the target takes an extra 1d6 fire damage.

### Ignite
As a bonus action, you can cause the blade to burst into flames. While ignited, the weapon sheds bright light in a 20-foot radius and dim light for an additional 20 feet. The flames last until you use a bonus action to extinguish them or until you are knocked unconscious.

### Fire Resistance
While attuned to this weapon, you have resistance to fire damage.

## Attunement

To attune to this weapon, you must spend a short rest meditating with the blade in your hands, during which you must make a DC 15 Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, you take 2d6 fire damage and cannot attune to the weapon until you complete a long rest.

## History

The Flameblade was forged by the ancient dwarven smith Thorgar Firebeard, who infused it with the essence of a fire elemental. Legend says that the blade was used to slay the dragon Ignis the Red, and the dragon's fiery breath was trapped within the metal itself.

## Variants

### Lesser Flameblade
*Weapon (longsword), uncommon*
A simpler version that deals only 1d4 extra fire damage and lacks the Ignite property.

### Greater Flameblade  
*Weapon (longsword), very rare*
An enhanced version that deals 2d6 extra fire damage and can cast *burning hands* once per day.
