# Spells

Custom spells and magical abilities for D&D 5e.

## Creating New Spells

To add a new spell:

1. Create a markdown file in the spells collection directory:
   - `dnd/_spells/spellname.md`
2. The page will automatically be accessible at `/dnd/spells/spellname/`
3. Update this overview page with a link to your new spell

## Spell Design Guidelines

When creating new spells:

- Follow D&D 5e spell level progression
- Consider spell school and components
- Balance damage/effects against existing spells
- Include clear casting time, range, and duration
- Provide interesting flavor and mechanics
