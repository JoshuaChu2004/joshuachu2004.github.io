# Phoenix's Nest of Everything
Welcome to my collection of D&D 5e homebrew content! Here you'll find custom classes, spells, items, races, and more that I've created for my campaigns.

## What You'll Find

- **Classes**: Custom subclasses and entirely new classes
- **Spells**: New magical incantations
- **Items**: Magic weapons, armor, and wondrous items
- **Races**: Custom player races and racial variants
- **Feats**: New character abilities and enhancements
- **House Rules**: Campaign-specific modifications and rulings
- **Reworks**: Changes to existing content, often to improve their mechanics or balance

## Browse Carefully!

Finished material will be denoted with a <i class="fa-solid fa-star"></i> symbol, signifying that it has gone through multiple iterations and has been playtested to a degree that I feel comfortable saying it is publishable material. If it doesn't have a :star:, it is either a work in progress or has not been playtested enough to be considered finished.

Despite this, everything here is still homebrew material. It is not endorsed by Wizards of the Coast, nor should you use it in any D&D setting without first getting the approval of the DM and the table.
