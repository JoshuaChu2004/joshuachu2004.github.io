***Forest spirits are animalistic souls created by an unknown entity, mostly rumored to be a powerful druid or a nature deity. They often do not take on the characteristics of a specific animal, but instead, many different ones in the region that they overlook. They are known to guard forests, protect nature from humanoid influence, and aid those who fall ill in their land. However, some experience a certain trauma or event that bestows the blessing and curse of a humanoid body.***

<div class="break"></div>

**Ability Score Increase.** Your Dexterity score increases by 2, and your Wisdom score increases by 1.

<div class="break"></div>

**Age.** Forest Spirits are immortal by nature and they are not born but created, thus their concept of age and also mental maturity is very fluid. Some forest spirits may have the curiosity of children yet have lived for thousands of years, while others may have ancient wisdom yet only have lived for decades.

<div class="break"></div>

**Alignment.** Forest Spirits are guardians of their home, making most of them Lawful by nature. Though if they are ever disconnected from that home or allowed to live free from it, they can take on personalities and ideals as diverse as any other mortal species.

<div class="break"></div>

**Size.** Forest Spirits in their humanoid form take on the looks of any humanoid with animalistic features such as antlers, horns, and hooves. Your size is Medium.

<div class="break"></div>

**Speed.** Your base walking speed is 30 feet.

<div class="break"></div>

**Keen Senses.** You gain proficiency on Perception checks.

<div class="break"></div>

**Darkvision.** You gain darkvision up to a range of 60 ft.

<div class="break"></div>

**Guardian of Beasts.** You are naturally able to speak and comprehend animals and other beasts.

<div class="break"></div>

**Fey Spirit.** Your creature type is Fey.

<div class="break"></div>

**Natural Nest.** By sleeping on foliage, you get the equivalent of a good night's sleep. However, you cannot gain a good night's sleep through a manmade bed.
