***Half humanoid, half dragon, born out of a love between a mortal and a member of the great drakes. Draconids possess the fearsome gift of shapeshifting into their draconic half. With the powerful presence of their draconic parent, they are often the emissaries of dragons, powerful rulers, or great warriors. But above all, they are different, special creatures, often raised in the comforts of mortal life but just as often treated as separate. Many Draconids spent their younger years coming to terms with their ancestry.***

<div class="break"></div>

**Ability Score Increase.** Your Charisma score increases by 2, and your Constitution score increases by 1.  

<div class="break"></div>

**Age.** Young Draconids grow quicker than their humanoid parent did, reaching physical adulthood between ages 14 and 18. However, their mental maturity is still the same as a normal humanoid. Draconids have the exact same life span as that of true dragons and either through ritual, gathering a hoard, or simply aging long enough, will reach their power as well.  

<div class="break"></div>

**Alignment.** Draconids fall squarely with many of the humanoids, being able to take on the temperaments that frequently permeate mortals. They are just as capable of evil as they are good, and may follow the laws pushed onto them as easily as they break them.  
<div class="break"></div>

**Size.** Draconids in their humanoid form take on the looks of a mix between their humanoid parent and the form their draconic parent took when they were conceived. Your size is Medium.  

<div class="break"></div>

**Speed.** Your base walking speed is 30 feet.  

<div class="break"></div>

**Languages.** You can read, speak, and write Common and Draconic.

<div class="break"></div>

**Draconic Lineage.** You are a direct descendant to a particular kind of dragon. Choose a type of dragon from the below list; this determines the damage and area of your breath weapon, and the type of resistance you gain. For your draconic abilities that require a saving throw, your DC is 8 + your Charisma modifier + your proficiency bonus.  

| Dracon Color | Damage Type | Breath Weapon   |
|--------------|-------------|-----------------|
| Black        | Acid        | Line (DEX save) |
| Blue         | Lightning   | Line (DEX save) |
| Brass        | Fire        | Line (DEX save) |
| Bronze       | Lightning   | Line (DEX save) |
| Copper       | Acid        | Line (DEX save) |
| Gold         | Fire        | Cone (DEX save) |
| Green        | Poison      | Cone (CON save) |
| Red          | Fire        | Cone (DEX save) |
| Silver       | Cold        | Cone (CON save) |
| White        | Cold        | Cone (CON save) |

**Damage Resistance.** You have resistance to the damage type associated with your ancestry.

<div class="break"></div>

**Draconic Form.** You can assume your draconic form as a bonus action, which lasts for 1 minute. When you do so, the following rules apply: 
- Your game statistics are replaced with the statistics of your draconic form, retaining your personality, and Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma scores, if they are higher than the draconic form. You also keep your skill and saving throw proficiencies. 
- At 1st level, your draconic form uses the <a href="#dragon-infant">Dragon Infant</a> statblock. When you reach 3rd level, you use the <a href="#dragon-wyrmling">Wyrmling</a> form when transforming and the transformation lasts for 10 minutes. At 7th lvl, you use the <a href="#dragon-young">Young</a> form and the transformation lasts for 1 hour. At 13th level, you use the <a href="#dragon-adult">Adult</a> form and the transformation lasts for 8 hours.
- Your draconic form shares the same Hit Point and Hit Point Maximum as you. When you transform, however, you gain temporary hit points equal to twice your level. 
- You revert to your humanoid form when you take a bonus action to do so, fall unconscious, drop below 0 hit points, or die.
- You can not cast spells nor use anything that requires hands, though you maintain concentration and may still use actions from ongoing spells. At 13th level, you may cast spells while in your draconic form.
- You are not able to make use of features from your class, feats, or items unless they are passive abilities (such as the Slasher feat) or ongoing effects.
- You choose whether your equipment falls to the ground in your space, merges into your new form, or is worn by it. Worn equipment functions as normal, but the DM decides whether it is practical for the new form to wear a piece of equipment, based on the creature's shape and size. Your equipment doesn't change size or shape to match the new form, and any equipment that the new form can't wear must either fall to the ground or merge with it. Equipment that merges with the form has no effect until you leave the form.

Once used, you can not transform into your draconic form again until the end of a long rest. You gain an additional use at 7th level.
