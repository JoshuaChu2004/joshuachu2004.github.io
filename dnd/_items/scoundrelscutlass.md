A curved, one-sided sword with a ruby embedded into the crossguard. When wielded, the user can hear the subtle sounds of water slapping the side of a ship. The cutlass has two charges. When you succeed a weapon attack with the sword, you may force the target to make a Constitution saving throw with a DC of 8 + your proficiency + your Dexterity. Upon fail, they are Blinded until the end of your next turn. You regain expended charges at the end of a long rest.
