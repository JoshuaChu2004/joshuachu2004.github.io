This pickaxe has a strange detachable head that is imbued with Aeromancy, allowing it to curve along the air like a boomerang. Whenever you fling the head, you may target 2 additional creatures if you can make a reasonable boomerang arc. This also allows you to strike targets around cover, though with disadvantage. The dagger will always find its way back to you if another creature has not caught it mid-flight.

This is also a +1 Magical Weapon.
