A handaxe made out of Ardite, a metal said to be blessed by a god. It is heavily sought after for its superior cutting edge over standard blades. The axe has 3 charges, which are regained at the end of a long rest.

<div class="break"></div>

**Bleeding Bite.** When you succeed an attack against a creature with this weapon, you may consume a charge to force the creature to make a Constitution saving throw with DC 14. On a fail, the wound ruptures open, causing them to Bleed for 1 minute.
