A revolver once belonging to Fandango. With a six-shot cylinder and magic imbued into its metal, it is able to augment the bullet in the chamber. Without an action, you may consume a charge and change the current bullet to one of the following shots.

<div class="break"></div>

**Flare Shot.** The bullet is transmutated to become a small bolt of fire, causing every creature within 10 ft of the target (and the target) to be set on fire. At the start of their turn, they take 8 Fire damage until they are put out.

<div class="break"></div>

**Pin Shot.** The bullet is made to stick the target into a nearby surface. When hit, the creature is sent backward 15 ft. sticking to any wall or hard surface encountered along the way, forcing them to make a Strength Save with DC 16. Upon fail, they are restrained. If there is no wall, they instead make a DEX Save with DC 14. Upon fail they are proned. The creature may reroll the save at the end of its turn.

<div class="break"></div>

**Fan the Hammer.** Continuously slam your palm on the hammer of the revolver to rapidly but inaccurately shoot multiple rounds. Make a number of attack rolls equal to how many shots you wish to fire, with a maximum being how many bullets are left in the cylinder. However, each subsequent shot after the first has an additional -1 to the hit (+0 on the first, -1 on the second, -2 on the third, etc.).

<div class="break"></div>

You have a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.
