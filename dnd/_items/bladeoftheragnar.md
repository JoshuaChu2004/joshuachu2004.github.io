A large longsword made of high-quality steel and leather. The soul of Ragnar rests within his old blade. While holding the sword, any creature may telepathically communicate with Ragnar, so long as they speak Common or Dwarvish. The sword has 3 charges that reset after a long rest which can be used for the following effect:

<div class="break"></div>

**Burning Blade.** You may cast the Imbue Flames spell by consuming a charge.

<div class="break"></div>

**Frenzied Strike.** When you reduce a creature to 0 hit points, you may consume a charge to make an additional attack as a bonus action.

<div class="break"></div>

You gain a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.

<div class="break"></div>

**Cursed - Ragnar's Soul.** While attuned to this sword, the spirit of Ragnar influences you. You are unable to run away or back down from combat or challenge.

<div class="break"></div>

**Cursed - Soulbound.** While attuned to this sword, you are unwilling to part with it and any attempts to have it be taken away from you are met with hostility.
