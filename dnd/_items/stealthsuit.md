A thin, nearly skin-tight suit that is meant to enhance the stealthy capabilities of the wearer. It was originally meant for a special task force in Averon, though the project was canceled after it was deemed a failure. Anyone who wears this suit will no longer have their clothes rustle or weapons make a sound when they move around, providing a +2 bonus to all Stealth rolls.
