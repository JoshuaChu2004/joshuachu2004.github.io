These twin daggers have a unique curviness to their design as well as Aeromancy imbued into their metal, allowing them to curve along the air like a boomerang. Whenever you throw the dagger, you may target 1 additional creature if you can make a reasonable boomerang arc. This also allows you to strike targets around cover, though with disadvantage. The dagger will always find its way back to you if another creature has not caught it mid-flight.

This is also a +1 Magical Weapon.
