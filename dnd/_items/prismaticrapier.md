A rapier forged from chalcum crystals, making the blade gleam and shine a dim light with a 10 ft. radius. The chalcum crystals also grant the rapier several magical abilities.

<div class="break"></div>

**Translucent Attacker.** Whenever you land a hit, you may use up a charge to become semi-transparent and make the Hide action with advantage for free. This translucence ends whenever you make an attack or cast a spell.

<div class="break"></div>

**Blinding Strike.** Whenever you land a hit, you may use up a charge to cause a bright light to be focused onto a creature's eyes, forcing them to make a CON save with DC 15 or be blinded. Creatures who have blindsight or do not rely on sight are immune to this effect.

<div class="break"></div>

**Critical Flash.** When landing a critical hit, the rapier suddenly unleashes a large amount of energy, dealing 1d8 radiant damage to the target.

<div class="break"></div>

This weapon is a +1 magical weapon.
