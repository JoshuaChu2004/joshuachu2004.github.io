A small rose-shaped medallion is connected by a small chain to form a necklace, given to you by Rose. It's said to be one of the last magical items in legal possession by a citizen of Avernoth. The charm is meant to protect to user from death or severe injury. Once per long rest, when the user drops to below 0 Hit Points, they stand back up with 1 Hit Point. Roll a d20. On a 1, the medallion shatters and loses its magic. The roll required to break the medallion increases by 1 each time it is used.
