The lantern you took from Galaerd and gave to SiDi is now empty, and its flame has snuffed out. With a special ritual spell, you can capture souls and place them into the lantern, upon which the green flame will relight. The lantern can hold up to four souls simultaneously, represented by a charge. As an action, you may consume a soul to roll on the Soul Table. Depending on the power of the soul consumed, it may grant you a modifier.

<div class="break"></div>

**Soul Table (1d10):**

| d10 | Effect |
|-----|--------|
|1| A creature within 30 ft. that can see the lantern gains 1d4 temporary hit points.|
|3| A creature within 30 ft. that can see the lantern gains advantage on its next attack roll, saving throw, or ability check.|
|4| Cause the soul to explode in a sonic shriek, casting Shatter within range.|
|5| The soul corrupts the heart of a creature, which must make a Wisdom saving throw with DC 14. Upon failure, they are affected as if by the Confusion spell.|
|6| A creature within 30 ft. is haunted by the soul you summoned and must make a Wisdom saving throw with DC 14 or be frightened until the end of your next turn.|
|7| Up to 3 creatures within 30 ft. that can see the lantern regain 1d8 hit points.|
|8| You tug on a number of souls' Direction equal to the modifier (min. 1), casting Command on all of them.|
|9| A creature within 30 ft. that can see the lantern upcasts their next spell by 1 level.|
|10| The soul projects itself in a location within 20 ft. of you for 1 minute. As a bonus action, you may swap places with it. On your turn, you may command the soul to move up to 15 ft.|
|11| The soul latches onto a creature within 30 ft. which must make a Strength saving throw with DC 15 or be restrained until the end of your next turn.|
|12| The soul expels its energy towards a creature, casting Blight within range.|
|13| A creature's Direction is empowered for 1 minute, granting them advantage on all attack rolls and ability checks.|
|14| The soul attempts to possess a creature, casting Dominate Person within range.|
|15| A creature within 30 ft. is invigorated, gaining the effects of the Haste spell for 3 rounds.|
|16| The soul shares the burden of a creature's Mind within 30 ft. for 1 minute. While they share a burden, the creature may hold concentration on 2 spells at once and has resistance to Psychic damage. However, taking Psychic damage instantly dispels the soul and one of the spells.|
|17| A creature within 30 ft. that can see the lantern has their Mind strengthened, giving it advantage on Wisdom, Intelligence, and Charisma saving throws for 1 minute.|
|18| A creature within 30 ft. that can see the lantern deals maximum damage on its next attack or spell.|
|19| The soul tears at the essence of a creature, casting Disintegrate.|
|20| Assume the statblock of the soul you captured until the end of your next turn. After the transformation ends, you are stunned for 1 round.|
