A thin, black cloak with a collar that rises up around the neck, it not only absorbs light, but it also exudes darkness around the wearer. The user is always considered Lightly Obscured unless the Daylight spell or other forms of radiant light is shone upon it.

<div class="break"></div>

The attuned user also gains the Darkness spell, which they are able to see through when they cast it. Once casted, this feature of the cloak can not be used until the next Dawn.
