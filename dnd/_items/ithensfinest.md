A highly engineered weapon, consisting of both a longsword and a pistol, allowing the user to wield them together as a single weapon or break them apart for two-weapon fighting. Only four of these weapons were every created, owned by the original designers, the Princes and Princesses of Ithen.

<div class="break"></div>

**Longsword.** A well-forged steel longsword, bearing the crest of Ithen. Heavily modified from a traditional longsword design, this weapon features an insert beneath the hilt for the sword's companion pistol.

<div class="break"></div>

**Pistol.** A handcrafted pistol, sporting a three-shot cyclic chamber. Ahead of its time, it utilizes bullets with a break-action design rather than a front-loading design. There is a specialized mechanism where the handle can be folded in to be inserted into its companion longsword. In front of the chamber, there are four sets of arcane runes that run along the barrel. However, three of them seem to be broken.

<div class="break"></div>

This weapon has two charges, which can be expended to augment a bullet as it leaves the chamber. When making a weapon attack with this pistol, you may expend a charge to use the following shot.

<div class="break"></div>

**Atlas's Orders.** On a hit, the target becomes marked with deadly energies until the start of your next turn. Once on each creature's turn, the target takes an additional d6 of damage when they take damage.

<div class="break"></div>

This pistol regains charges at the end of a long rest.
