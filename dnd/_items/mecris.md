A slim staff carved from a grey wood which originates from the Fey Wilds. According to The Merchant, it is one of the last artifacts from the locked land. Sitting at the top of the staff is a small green gem, which has markings within it that makes it look like an eye. The staff uses the wielder's mind to swing the weapon, rather than their strength or dexterity. Furthermore, the user is able to channel the lost power of the Fey Wild through the weapon in the form of casting spells after a successful hit. The staff can be strengthened after being dipped in springs that leaked into the material plane from the Fey Wild ages ago.

<div class="break"></div>

**Mind Feed.** Whenever you successfully attack a hostile creature, you gain a +1 bonus to spell attack rolls and saving throw DCs of your druid spells. This stacks to a maximum of 3. Any bonuses past +1 only last for 1 minute.

<div class="break"></div>

**Channel Fey.** Upon a successful attack, you may cast any of these spells for no action, consuming a number of charges equal to the spell's level.

<div class="break"></div>

**Channel Fey Spells:**
- **Maddening Forest.** 1st level
- **Whisper of Nature.** 1st level
- **Leech Life.** 2nd level
- **Mark of Ceres.** 3rd level

This weapon is a +1 Magical Weapon.
