A short bow made of wood from a far-off land, it has been imported into nations around the world. Its string is enchanted to release extreme shocks of electricity, making it an excellent tool in restraining targets.

<div class="break"></div>

**Static Charge.** After hitting a target, the arrow releases forks of lighting dealing 1d6 lightning damage and making the target roll a Constitution save with DC 14, or else it is left Paralyzed. The lightning will then attempt to find other targets. The attuned may choose another target within 10 ft. who also takes 1d6 lightning damage and makes a Constitution save or be Paralyzed. This can be repeated once more to Paralyze a total of 3 targets. At the start of the target's turn, it must make another Constitution save to end the Paralysis, with the DC lowering by 1 for every subsequent roll (14, 13, 12...).

<div class="break"></div>

**Lightningrod.** The bow gains 1 extra charge if the user is struck with lightning damage.
