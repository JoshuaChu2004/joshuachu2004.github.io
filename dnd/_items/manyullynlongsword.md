A longsword made out of Manyullyn, a metal said to be blessed by a goddess. It is heavily sought after for its consistency and lightweight form.

<div class="break"></div>

**Sweeping Strike.** Using the power of the blessed metal, magically extend your weapon's reach so it hits all creatures within 5 ft. of the target.
