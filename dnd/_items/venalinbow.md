A magical shortbow crafted from Venalin, a metal said to be blessed by a god.. It is commonly seen among the ranks of archers, but it is also seen in the hands of basic adventurers, who either bought the sacred weapon or looted it from another location. It is famous for its ability to fire arrows straighter and faster than any standard bow.

<div class="break"></div>

**Moonlit Arrow.** Once per long rest, a nocked arrow can be infused with the light of the Moon, increasing its speed and strength. The user has advantage on their shot while also dealing an extra 1d6 radiant damage. The damage is increased by 5 if it is used at night.
