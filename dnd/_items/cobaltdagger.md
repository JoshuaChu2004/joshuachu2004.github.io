A dagger made out of Cobalt, a metal said to be blessed by a god. It is heavily sought after for its lightweight and similar cutting power. The dagger has 3 charges, which are regained at the end of a long rest.

<div class="break"></div>

**Swift Strike.** When you make an attack, you may consume a charge to make an additional attack with this weapon as a bonus action.
