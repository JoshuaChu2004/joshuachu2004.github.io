A mask crafted or forged out of a durable material, bearing the symbol to represent the Vigilante who wears it. While you wear the mask, you gain a bonus to Vigilante save DC of your Vigilante abilities. The bonus is determined by the mask's rarity.

<div class="break"></div>

You can also restore up to half (rounded down) of your Modus Points as a bonus action. Once used, this feature can not be used again until the end of your next long rest.
