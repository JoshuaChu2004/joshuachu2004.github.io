A pick for a stringed instrument with two rings to insert the user's fingers. The pick is charged with sonic energy that allows it to split sound waves in two. When you cast a bard spell that targets a single creature or use bardic inspiration, you may use a charge to target an additional creature. This magical item regains its charges at the end of a long rest.
