Knuckles infused with the essence of wind, allowing the user to imbue their fists with the momentum of their speed, causing destructive effects on their targets.

<div class="break"></div>

While attuned, the user's speed increases by 10 ft. Your unarmed strikes become +2.

<div class="break"></div>

**Consume Momentum.** You may use up some of your movement in exchange for more damage when making an unarmed strike. For every 5 ft. of movement used, your unarmed strike deals an additional d4 of bludgeoning damage.

<div class="break"></div>

**Wind Cloak.** Stir up massive winds that kick up dust and debris around you, allowing you to make the Hide action as a bonus action. You stay hidden until you choose to dispel the winds or make an Attack action.
