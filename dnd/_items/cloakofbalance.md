A cloak made out of a fine cloth that is a deep purple. It is accented in black with holy symbols of Ianite. The cloak itself is blessed by Ianite to bear a fraction of her divine might. The cloak was once very common when Ruxomar was still a kingdom but has slowly become rarer and rarer as time passes.

You can now cast the Judgement and Repulse spell at will.
