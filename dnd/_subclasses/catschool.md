***Witchers of the School of the Cat are notorious for their slyness and willingness to take on humanoid contracts. To them, coin is coin and people can be more monstrous than the monsters themselves. Imbued with augmented mutations to allow for silent movement, their Cat medallion is a sight most hope to never see.***

| Witcher Level | Features |
|---------------|----------|
| 3rd           | Feline Discipline |
| 7th           | Silver for Monsters |
| 11th          | Evasion |
| 15th          | Steel for Humanoids |
| 18th          | Path of the Cat |

### Feline Discipline
At 3rd level, the School of the Cat has trained you to be a silent killer. Your critical hit range is reduced by 1. Also, you may use the Hide action as a bonus action.

### Silver for Monsters
At 7th level, the additional mutations from your school continue to adjust the biochemistry of your body. You may choose two additional Mutations at this level. Also, when you land a critical hit, you may spend an Adrenaline point to add an additional d8 to the damage roll.
Once per round, you gain an Adrenaline point when you make an attack with advantage.

### Evasion
At 11th level, your body naturally contorts itself to escape dangerous effects. When you use Reactive Slip against an effect that had you roll a Dexterity Saving throw, you now take no damage if you had succeeded the saving throw.

### Steel for Humanoids
At 15th level, your Signs is often followed by a flurry of precise strikes. Once per turn, when a creature fails a saving throw against one of your Signs, you may spend an Adrenaline point to increase your critical hit range by 1 against that creature for 1 minute. You may only have this effect active on one creature at once.

### Path of the Cat
At 18th level, you have become a master of the shadows and blade, being a fearful presence even if you are not there. When you succeed a Stealth check, you may spend an Adrenaline point to force a creature to make a Wisdom saving throw against your Adrenaline Save DC. On a failure, they are frightened for 1 minute.

You also gain advantage on Stealth checks.
