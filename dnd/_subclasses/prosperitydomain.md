# Prosperity Domain
***Clerics of the domain of Prosperity serve deities who emoby wealth, fortune, and the flourishing of communities. They see prosperity as not only the accumulation of gold and materials, but in the growth of knowledge, opportunity, and quality of life. These clerics are often educators, advisors, and merchants, guiding others through wise investments and cooperation. However, not all of them are generous volunteers or philanthropists, but rather see the path to prosperity through the exchange of goods and services. Through their divine acts, they collect a payment in some form to ensure that currency continues to flow. Many Prosperity clerics look down upon those that seek to hoard wealth and good to themselves, denying others the chance to flourish. To such greed, they bring the wrath of their deity for their transgressions.***

### Pay it Forward
Starting at 1st level, your god grants you an allowance of magical gold pieces to channel divine power through. At the end of a long rest, you gain a number of temporary gold pieces equal to ten times your cleric level. These gold pieces appear just like real ones and can be used as material components for spells, crafting supplies, and features that require gold. However, they begin to corrode and turn into stone pebbles within seconds if you attempt to exchange it to others for goods and services. Unused temporarily gold pieces are lost at the end of a long rest.


### Welfare
Also at 1st level, you may exchange gold to aid your allies. As a bonus action, choose a creature you can see within 30 ft. and expend 5 gold pieces to choose one of the following benefits:
- The target gains 1d6 + your Wisdom modifier temporary hit points for 1 minute.
- The target adds your Wisdom modifier to their next ability check, attack roll, or saving throw within 1 minute.
- The target gains a reflective shield for 1 minute. Once per turn, when a creature hits the target with an attack, the attacker takes radiant damage equal to your Wisdom modifier.

### Channel Divinity: Prosperity’s Hope
Starting at 2nd level, you can use your Channel Divinity to imbue your allies with hope for a brighter future. As an action, choose a number of creatures within 30 feet of you equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum 1). For 1 minute, each chosen creature can use the Help action as a bonus action. In addition, when a chosen creature uses the Help action, it can also grant advantage on the target’s next saving throw instead of the usual benefits.

### Improved Welfare
At 6th level, your ability to aid others has strengthened. When you use the Welfare feature, you may instead expend 10 gold pieces to choose one of the following benefits:
- The target’s next attack that succeeds deals an additional 1d10 + your Wisdom modifier radiant damage for 1 minute.
- The target can immediately use their reaction to move up to five times your Wisdom modifier in feet without provoking opportunity attacks.
- The target gains proficiency in a saving throw of your choice for 1 minute. If you grant this benefit again while another creature already has it, the original effect ends.

### Gilded Spellcasting
Starting at 8th level, you can infuse your spells with divine wealth. Once on each of your turns when you cast a spell that deals damage, you can expend 15 gold pieces to add radiant damage equal to your Wisdom modifier to one damage roll of that spell.

Beginning at 14th level, this extra damage increases by 1d8.

### Valley of Plenty
Starting at 17th level, you are imbued with a shard of your deity’s divinity. When you or a willing creature within 30 feet of you that you can see rolls dice for damage or healing, you can choose to reroll any number of those dice. For each die you reroll, you must immediately expend 5 gold pieces. You must use the new rolls.
