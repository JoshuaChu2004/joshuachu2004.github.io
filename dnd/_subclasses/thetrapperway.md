# Way of the Trapper
***Monks of the Way of the Trapper are masters at manipulating the limbs and bodies of their opponents as well as delivering powerful and precise blows to stagger their enemies. While other schools of Monks frown on their more brutal approach to combat, Trapper Monks are ready to do what is necessary.***

| Monk Level | Features |
|------------|--------|
| 3rd        | Trapper Technique, Stagger |
| 6th        | Takedown |
| 11th       | Unrelenting Offense |
| 17th       | Unwavering Defense |

### Trapper Technique
Starting when you choose this tradition at 3rd level, you become well versed in Trapping, a form of blocking and dodging that positions your opponents so that they are vulnerable to retaliation. Whenever an enemy misses a Melee Attack against you, you may spend a ki point to make a Grapple or Shove as a reaction. If you have taken the Dodge action on your turn, you do not spend a ki point.

### Stagger
Also when you choose this tradition at 3rd level, you learn to place your strikes to Stagger your opponents. Once per turn when you succeed an unarmed strike, you may inflict the Staggered condition on your target until the end of your next turn. If you succeed an unarmed strike and have already Staggered a creature this turn, you may spend a ki point to Stagger your target.

Staggered creatures have their movement decreased by 10 ft. When you hit a Staggered creature with an unarmed strike, you may end the condition to Grapple or Shove without using an action. take the Grapple or Shove action When you Shove a creature in this way to push them, you may move them 10 ft. in any direction.

### Takedown
At 6th level, you are able to perform takedowns on Staggered opponents. When you succeed on an Unarmed Strike against a Staggered creature, the creature makes a Strength Saving Throw. Creatures that are grappled by you have disadvantage on the saving throw. Upon fail, you may spend two ki points to perform a Takedown. After a Takedown, the creature is no longer Staggered. 

- **Crooked Foot.** Creature is struck in the stomach before being swept from under it, causing it to fall prone.  Roll an additional martial arts die for damage.
- **Flying Kick.** Creature is launched backwards by an airborne kick, sending it 15 ft. away. Roll an additional martial arts die for damage.
- **Discombobulate.** Creature’s sensory organs are targeted, causing them to be Deafened or Blinded for 1 minute. Roll an additional martial arts die for damage. They reroll the save at the end of their turn.
- **Face Smash.** Creature’s head is slammed into a solid object, causing them to become Dazed until the end of your next turn.
- **Cripple Ankle.** Creature’s ankle or leg is traumatized, causing them to have disadvantage on all Dexterity Saving Throws until the end of your next turn. Roll an additional martial arts die for damage.
- **Arm Break.** One of the Creature’s attacking limbs is broken. If they make an attack with that arm or use the arm in any way, they take 1d6 slashing damage. This effect ends when they are healed.
- **Humiliating Feint.** Creature is threatened by a devastating attack before being light tapped on the back of the head. Until the end of the creature’s next turn, it must attempt to spend its turn to harm you in some way.

### Unrelenting Offense
At 11th level, your Staggering blows are more devastating than before. Staggered creatures now have disadvantage on Strength checks and Strength Saving Throws.

### Unwavering Defense
At 17th level, your defense is no longer interrupted when attacked. Whenever you use your reaction to make an Unarmed Strike, Grapple, or Shove action and succeed, you may spend a ki point to regain your reaction.
