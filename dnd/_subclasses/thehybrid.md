***Your patron is a War Devil, a powerful Fiend with the capability to turn anything into a weapon. In an attempt to dominate your psyche to be on the material plane once again, it has created a botched fusing of your minds. Despite this, the Devil is still able to take control of your body whenever it pleases, outside of rare, exceptional circumstances. Through this uneasy alliance, Hybrid warlocks roam the world following the whims of their Devil, often fighting for more control in any way they can.***

| Warlock Level | Features |
|----------------|--------|
| 1st            | Devilish Transformation, Devil Charges, Hell’s Boons, Demonic Aggression |
| 6th            | Rapid Recovery, Fiendish Charm |
| 10th           | Form to Facade, Flesh to Fire |
| 14th           | Freedom of Mind and Body, Return to Power |

### Expanded Spell List
The Hybrid lets you choose from an expanded list of spells when you learn a warlock spell. The following spells are added to the warlock spell list for you.

#### Hybrid Spells Table

| Warlock Level  | Spells |
|----------------|--------|
| 1st            | Cause Fear, Zephyr Strike |
| 3rd            | Borrowed Knowledge, Magic Weapon |
| 5th            | Speak with Dead, Blinding Smite |
| 7th            | Fabricate, Staggering Smite |
| 9th            | Modify Memory, Steel Wind Strike |

### Devilish Transformation
Starting at 1st level, your patron will occasionally take and relinquish control from you to forward its own agenda. As an Action, you may switch between your Devil form and your Original form. There is not necessarily a visual difference between the two, though your patron will almost always carry a different personality and way of thinking. Depending on your form, you will have access to different abilities and features from this class.

### Devil Charges
Also at 1st level, you have access to Devil Charges, which are used to fuel your hellish abilities. You have three Devil Charges, which are regained at the end of a short rest. You may consume a Devil Charge to switch between your forms as a bonus action.

You gain an additional Devil Charge at 6th, 10th, and 14th level.

### Hell’s Boons
Also at 1st level, some of your patron’s powers are still available to you when they are not in control. While in your Original form, consume a Devil Charge when using any of the following abilities:
- **Ashen Ward.** As a reaction to you or an ally within 20 ft. taking damage, reduce the damage taken by 1d6 + your proficiency bonus + half of your Warlock level (rounded down).
- **Surpassed Limitation.** Gain advantage on an ability check that you are proficient in. This must be done before rolling.
- **Shadow Step.** As a bonus action, teleport to an unoccupied space within a number of feet equal to five times your proficiency bonus. If the space you appear in is Lightly Obscured, you may immediately make a Dexterity (Stealth) check to hide.

### Demonic Aggression
Also at 1st level, your patron uses your body to wreak havoc and death upon their enemies. While in your Devil form, consume a Devil Charge to gain one of the following benefits:
- **Agonizing Wound.** As a bonus action, add half your Charisma modifier to all weapon and spell damage rolls until the end of your next turn. You do not add your Charisma modifier to a damage roll a second time if another feature already tells you to.
- **Reaping Vengeance.** As a reaction to a creature hitting you with an attack, make a weapon attack against them. On hit, regain hit points equal to half the damage you deal to them.
- **Leaping Strike.** In place of a weapon attack, jump up to an unoccupied space within a number of feet equal to five times your proficiency bonus. If the space you land in has a creature within melee range, you may immediately make a weapon attack against them.

### Rapid Recovery
At 6th level, your patron needs your body intact while not being used by them. While in your Original form, you can consume a Devil Charge to begin focusing your energy to mend your wounds. You must concentrate on this as if concentrating on a spell for 1 minute. Once complete, you may roll a number of Hit Die equal to your proficiency bonus and regain that many hit points.

### Fiendish Charm
At 6th level, your patron can begin placing influence on the others you surround yourself with. While in your Devil form, you can consume a Devil Charge to force a humanoid within 30 ft. you can see to make a Wisdom saving throw or be magically charmed for 1 day as an action. The charmed target obeys your spoken commands. If the target suffers any harm from you or another creature or receives a suicidal command from you, the target can repeat the saving throw, ending the effect on itself on a success. If a target's saving throw is successful, or if the effect ends for it, the creature is immune to your Fiendish Charm for the next 24 hours.

### Form to Facade
At 10th level, while in your Original form, you learn the Mislead spell. It counts as a warlock spell for you, but it doesn’t count against the number of spells you know. You can also cast it once using a Devil Charge without consuming a spell slot, and you regain the ability to do so when you finish a long rest. 

### Flesh to Fire
At 10th level, while in your Devil form, you learn the Immolation spell. It counts as a warlock spell for you, but it doesn’t count against the number of spells you know. You can also cast it once using a Devil Charge without consuming a spell slot, and you regain the ability to do so when you finish a long rest. 

### Freedom of Mind and Body
Starting when you reach 14th level, either you have harnessed leverage over your patron or a new, fairer deal has been struck between you, granting greater control over your abilities. While in your Original form, you may use any of your abilities limited to the Devil form, though they cost an additional Devil Charge. Also, when you are concentrating on a spell, you reduce all damage taken by your Charisma modifier.

### Return to Power
Also when you reach 14th level, your patron can temporarily harness the power they once had. While in your Devil Form, you can consume a Devil Charge to undergo a true Devilish Transformation for 1 minute as an action. While in your True Devil form, you can not switch to your Original form and you only revert to your Devil form when a minute passes, you fall unconscious, or die. When you transform, all creatures of your choice within 60 ft. that can see you must make a Wisdom saving throw or be Frightened until the end of your transformation. During your transformation, you have advantage on attacks against Frightened creatures and they have disadvantage on saving throws against your Spell Save DC. A creature may reroll the saving throw at the end of their turn to end the condition.
