# Chronoshifter
***Your blood carries a special trait that allows you to subtly adjust the weave of time. Rare as it is dangerous to be able to perform such feats, Chronoshifters must be careful in the use of their magic. Many hide their abilities, relegating them to small, inconsequential adjustments while the ambitious utilize them to change the world.***

| Sorcerer Level | Features |
|----------------|--------|
| 1st            | Flow of Time, Timeline Desynchronization, Alter Flow |
| 6th            | Disrupt Timestream   |
| 14th           | Temporal Takeback |
| 18th           | Time Lord |

### Flow of Time
You learn additional spells when you reach certain levels in this class, as shown on the Time Spells table. Each of these spells counts as a sorcerer spell for you, but it doesn’t count against the number of sorcerer spells you know.

### Time Spells Table

| Sorcerer Level | Spells |
|----------------|--------|
| 1st            | Temporal Peace, Bullet Time |
| 3rd            | Flash of Steel, Hold Person |
| 5th            | Desynchronize, Slow |
| 7th            | Recall, Divination |
| 9th            | Supercede, Temporal Shunt |

### Timeline Desynchronization
You innately have the ability to subtly affect the flow of time, even reversing in small bursts. However, using these abilities causes destabilization of the time stream, which can lead to devastating results.  

<div class="break"> </div>

At 1st level, you have two Temporal points, which recharge after a long rest. If you do not have any Temporal points, you may still utilize abilities that need them, but you must roll on the Desynchronization Table. You add a modifier to the roll equal to the number of times you’ve rolled on the table since your last long rest. If you cause other creatures to roll on the Desynchronization table, they make an unmodified roll unless stated otherwise, using your level for any effects. You gain an additional Temporal point at 6th, 14th, and 18th level.
#### Desynchronization Table

| d8 | Desynchronization Effect |
|----|--------------------------|
| 1  | You hear feedback in your mind, take 1d4 + your level psychic damage. |
| 2  | You are slightly disoriented, halving your movement speed until the end of your next turn. |
| 3  | Your eyes blur as your sight shifts between points in time, blinding you for 1 minute. |
| 4  | You suddenly feel sick from the whiplash of traveling through time, poisoning you until the end of a long rest. |
| 5  | You feel a psychic tear in your mind as it struggles to adjust to the timestream, take 2d8 + your level psychic damage. |
| 6  | You are dazed until the end of your next turn as you lose grip on your time stream. |
| 7  | You cause a ripple in time as creatures and the area around you are slightly altered, oftentimes detrimental to your goals. Creatures might be more hostile, environments more deadly, or a new enemy may appear in the midst of a battle. Only you are aware of the change. |
| 8  | The area around you is suddenly desynchronized from the timeline, causing all creatures within 10 ft. to make an unmodified roll on this table, ignoring this result on subsequent rolls. |

You gain an extra Temporal point at 6th, 14th, and 18th level.

### Alter Flow
Also at 1st level, you are able to adjust the time stream of creatures and solid objects in motion. Allowing you to do the following:  

- **Freeze Object.** As a reaction to an object(s) weighing no more than fifty times your level in pounds and within 120 ft. moving, spend a Temporal point to stop its position in place for 1 minute. You can force a creature attempting to move the object to make a Strength check against your Spell Save DC. On a fail, the object remains in place.

- **Adjust Creature.** As a bonus action, spend a Temporal point to cause a creature within 120 ft. to experience time slower or quicker for 1 minute. An unwilling creature must make a Wisdom saving throw against your Spell Save DC to avoid the effect.
    - **Slower Perception.** Grant the creature advantage on Dexterity checks and saving throws, a bonus to To Hit rolls equal to your proficiency bonus, or increase their movement equal to five times your proficiency bonus.
    - **Quicker Perception.** Grant the creature disadvantage on Dexterity checks and saving throws, a minus to To Hit rolls equal to your proficiency bonus, or decrease their movement equal to five times your proficiency bonus (to a minimum of 5 ft.).

### Disrupt Timestream
At 6th level, you can force a creature to desynchronize from the timeline. When a creature is affected by one of your spells, you may spend a Sorcery point to force it to roll on the Desynchronization table. You may now also use Temporal Points in place of Sorcery points, but not the other way around.

### Temporal Takeback
At 14th level, you may spend a Temporal point to reverse time in a localized area by a few seconds, reversing the effects of an action, bonus action, or section of conversation. You may spend three sorcery points to make a number of creatures up to your Charisma modifier not remember the events that were reversed, otherwise all creatures remember. Once this feature is used, it can not be used again for 1 minute.

### Time Lord
Beginning at 18th level, you are able to manipulate time on an extreme scale. Over the course of 1 hour, you can perform a ritual to spend 10 Temporal points to reverse time globally by up to 1 day, allowing yourself and a number of creatures up to your Charisma modifier to remember the events of that day. Once this feature is used, you can not use it again for 1 week.

<div class="break"> </div>

Alternatively, you can reverse the time of an area within 1 mile by up to 1 year. Creatures are unaffected by this alternative effect and creatures that can see the area can witness the time shift. Areas that were affected by this feature can not be reversed again for 5 years.
