***They say that love can be as powerful a magic as the most destructive of spells. Soulmate warlocks are those who made a pact with a loved one who has already passed. But despite the circumstances, the bond between them manifests into potent magic. Out of devotion and love to their patron, these warlocks look onto the future with hope, knowing that they are never truly alone.***

| Warlock Level  | Features |
|----------------|--------|
| 1st            | Bonus Cantrips, Precious Keepsake |
| 6th            | Love’s Embrace |
| 10th           | In Sickness and in Health |
| 14th           | Not Even Death Do We Part |

### Expanded Spell List

The Soulmate lets you choose from an expanded list of spells when you learn a warlock spell. The following spells are added to the warlock spell list for you.

#### Soulmate Spells Table

| Warlock Level  | Spells |
|----------------|--------|
| 1st            | Guiding Bolt, Feather Fall |
| 3rd            | Augury, Enhance Ability |
| 5th            | Fly, Spirit Guardians |
| 7th            | Death Ward, Divination |
| 9th            | Rary's Telepathic Bond, Wall of Light |

### Bonus Cantrips
At 1st level, you learn the Guidance and Spare the Dying cantrips. They count as warlock cantrips for you, but they don’t count against your number of cantrips known.

### Precious Keepsake
At 1st level, you have a keepsake of some form from your soulmate that strengthens your connection to them. It could be a piece of jewelry, an article of clothing, or even a weapon. No matter its form, you may use it as a spellcasting focus for your warlock spells. The keepsake has Maximum Hit Points equal to five times your warlock level. While wearing or holding the keepsake, you gain the following benefits:
- **Shared Experience.** You gain proficiency in one skill of your choice. This represents a skill your soulmate had mastered and now shares with you.
- **Fleet of Foot.** Your movement speed increases by 5 ft.
- **Flickering Memory.** When you make an Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma ability check, you can choose to reroll the die. You must use the new result. The keepsake takes 5 damage when you do so.
- **Guardian Bond.** When a creature you can see within 30 feet takes damage, you can use your reaction to reduce the damage by 1d8 + your Charisma modifier + half your warlock level (rounded down, minimum 1). The keepsake takes damage equal to the amount reduced.
When the keepsake is reduced to 0 Hit Points, it becomes damaged and you lose all of the benefits. The keepsake recovers its Hit Points at the end of a long rest.

### Love’s Embrace
At 6th level, you have the ability to call upon your bond to aid others. Whenever you use a Warlock pact slot to cast a spell, you may cause a number of creatures (including yourself) equal to the slot’s level within 30 ft. that you can see to gain 1d8 + your Charisma modifier temporary hit points.

### In Sickness and in Health
At 10th level, when your health wanes, your keepsake grows stronger to end the threats to your life. When you are below half your hit point maximum and you cast a damaging warlock spell, you can roll one additional damage die of the spell’s primary damage type and add it to the total. The keepsake takes damage equal to the amount added.

### Not Even Death Do We Part
At 14th level, the bond between you and your soulmate endures across time, distance, and death itself. Your keepsake regains 10 hit points every hour, even if it is reduced to 0 hit points.
