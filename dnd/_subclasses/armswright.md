### Master of Arms
Starting at 3rd level, you gain proficiency in martial weapons and firearms. You also gain a fighting style.
- **Archery (PHB).** You gain a +2 bonus to attack rolls you make with ranged weapons.
- **Blind Fighting (TCE).** You have blindsight with a range of 10 feet. Within that range, you can effectively see anything that isn't behind total cover, even if you're blinded or in darkness. Moreover, you can see an invisible creature within that range, unless the creature successfully hides from you.
- **Dueling (PHB).** When you are wielding a melee weapon in one hand and no other weapons, you gain a +2 bonus to damage rolls with that weapon.
- **Great Weapon Fighting (PHB).** When you roll a 1 or 2 on a damage die for an attack you make with a melee weapon that you are wielding with two hands, you can reroll the die and must use the new roll, even if the new roll is a 1 or a 2. The weapon must have the two-handed or versatile property for you to gain this benefit.
- **Interception (TCE).** When a creature you can see hits a target, other than you, within 5 feet of you with an attack, you can use your reaction to reduce the damage the target takes by 1d10 + your proficiency bonus (to a minimum of 0 damage). You must be wielding a shield or a simple or martial weapon to use this reaction.
- **Protection (PHB).** When a creature you can see attacks a target other than you that is within 5 feet of you, you can use your reaction to impose disadvantage on the attack roll. You must be wielding a shield.
- **Thrown Weapon Fighting (TCE).** You can draw a weapon that has the thrown property as part of the attack you make with the weapon.
    - In addition, when you hit with a ranged attack using a thrown weapon, you gain a +2 bonus to the damage roll.
- **Two-Weapon Fighting (PHB).** When you engage in two-weapon fighting, you can add your ability modifier to the damage of the second attack.


### Magnum Opus
Starting at 3rd level, your inventive pursuits has pushed you to endlessly toil over one project, your Magnum Opus. At the end of a long rest, you can turn a weapon into your Magnum Opus, provided you have tinker’s tools in hand.
Your Magnum Opus gains the following benefits:
- You can use the weapon as a spellcasting focus for your artificer spells.
- You can apply your artificer infusions to the weapon even if it is already magical. If your infusion grants a bonus to the weapon’s to hit and damage rolls while the wepon already has one, it takes the higher of the two.
- When you attack with the weapon, you can add your Intelligence modifier, instead of Strength or Dexterity, to the attack and damage rolls.

### Weapon Pattern
Also at 3rd level, you have a greater degree of customization for your Magnum Opus. Choose one of the following weapon patterns:  Elemental, Break, or Adapting.

You can change the weapon’s pattern whenever you finish a long rest, provided you have tinker’s tools in hand.

**Elemental.** You have augmented your weapon to unleash deadly magic when used. Choose one of the following damage types: acid, cold, fire, lightning, or thunder. It gains the following features:
- The weapon deals an additional d4 of your chosen type’s damage. 
- When you make an attack with the weapon, you may cast an artificer cantrip that deals your chosen damage type as a bonus action.

**Break.** You have magically integrated a second weapon that lacks the Two-Handed property or a shield into the form of your Magnum Opus, which can break apart to wield the items separately. It gains the following features:
- You can break apart your Magnum Opus as a bonus action, wielding the weapons in both hands. You may also reform them as a bonus action.
- The two weapons are still separate items for the purposes of your Infuse Items feature, but if both weapons require attunement, they only take up one attunement slot. Also the maximum number of items you can infuse at once increases by 1, but that extra item must be a part of your Magnum Opus.
    - **Combined Form.** While combined, your Magnum Opus gains the Two-Handed property and retains the traits of your primary weapon. As a bonus action, you can activate the properties of your secondary weapon:
    - **Polearm.** Shove a creature within 5 ft.
    - **Sword or Dagger.** You can make one attack with the Magnum Opus as a bonus action. This attack uses a d6 damage die, regardless of the weapon’s normal damage.
    - **Hammer or Axe.** Attempt to grapple a creature within 5 feet.
    - **Thrown or Ranged Weapon.** You can make a single ranged attack with the Magnum Opus as a bonus action. This attack uses a d6 damage die, regardless of the weapon’s normal damage.
    - **Shield.** Gain +2 AC until the start of your next turn.
- While broken, you have a unique synergy with your secondary weapon. Depending on what your secondary weapon is, you gain one of the following reactions:
    - **Polearm.** As a reaction to a creature within 5 ft. succeeding an attack, you may make a Grapple action against them.
    - **Sword or Dagger.** As a reaction to a creature succeeding an attack against you, you may reduce the damage by 1d6 + your proficiency bonus.
    - **Hammer or Axe.** As a reaction to a creature missing an attack against you, you may make a Shove action against them.
    - **Thrown or Ranged Weapon.** As a reaction to a creature missing a melee attack against you, you may make a weapon attack against them with your secondary weapon.
    - **Shield.** As a reaction to a creature within 5 ft. of you being attacked, change the target of the attack to yourself. This must be done before the attack has been rolled.

**Adapting.** You have prepared your Magnum Opus to be ready for nearly every situation, allowing it to change and shift to suit your needs.
- As a bonus action, you can transform your Magnum Opus into a different weapon type with which you are proficient. The weapon retains all of your artificer infusions. The first time you hit a creature with the new weapon within 1 minute, the target must succeed on a Strength saving throw or suffer one of the following effects, determined by the weapon’s new form:
    - **Polearm.** The creature is knocked Prone
    - **Sword or Dagger.** The creature is inflicted with Bleeding for 1 minute.
    - **Bludgeoning Weapon or Axe.** The creature has disadvantage on attack rolls until the end of their next turn.
    - **Thrown or Ranged Weapon.** The creature’s movement is reduced to 0 until the end of their next turn.
- As an action, you can replace one of your artificer infusions on the Magnum Opus with a different infusion you know. The new infusion lasts for 1 minute, after which the weapon reverts to the previous infusion.

### Extra Attack
Starting at 5th level, you can attack twice, rather than once, whenever you take the Attack action on your turn.

### Improved Pattern
Also at 9th level, you have refined your Magnum Opus, unlocking new power and abilities. Your chosen Pattern gains an additional effect:
- **Elemental.** The extra damage die of your Magnum Opus increases to a d6. Also, when you cast a cantrip as a bonus action, you may add your Intelligence modifier to the damage roll.
- **Break.** When you split your weapons as a bonus action, you can make an attack with your secondary weapon as part of the same action.
- **Adapting.** When you use replace an artificer infusion, your next attack with the weapon has advantage.


### Masterwork Weapon
Starting at 15th level, you have reached the pinnacle of your craft, granting you the following benefits:
- **Elemental.** The extra damage die of your Magnum Opus increases to a d8. Also, instead of casting a cantrip, you may cast an artificer spell as a bonus action after making a weapon attack with your Magnum Opus. The spell still has to deal your chosen damage type.
- **Break.** When you split or combine your weapons as a bonus action, your next attack within 1 minute has advantage.
- **Adapting.** Creatures have disadvantage saving throws against the effects of your Adapting Magnum Opus.

## Additional Artificer Infusion Options

### Improved Sights
***Item: A simple or martial weapon with the ammunition property (requires attunement)*** 

This magic weapon grants a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with it when it's used to make a ranged attack. Also, the weapon’s normal and long ranges are increased by 10 ft, and it no longer has disadvantage when making attacks against a target within 5 ft.
