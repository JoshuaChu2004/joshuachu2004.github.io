***Within the walls of civilization, the fields of farms, and the trees of the wilderness, monsters threatens the lives of people. Whether through trickery, charms, or sheer brutality, they feast on populations and bring settlements to ruin. Against these horrors stand the Slayers, the final line of defense for those who can not protect themselves. Trained through relentless discipline and armed with specialized breathing techniques, these alone can cut down the mightiest of foes. The path of a Slayer is one filled with tragedy and death, with many Slayers falling long before they grow old. However, it is a calling they greet with grim resolve and one they fight for till their final breath.***

### Breathing Style
You have trained to hone your breathing in a certain way to grant your body the strength and speed to perform Breathing Techniques. At 3rd level, choose one of the following Breathing Styles: Sun, Moon, Water, Fire, Thunder, Wind, or Beast. 

<div class="break"></div>

At 3rd level, you learn two Breathing Techniques from the style’s list, which take the form of spells. These techniques count as ranger spells for you, but do not count against the number of ranger spells you may know. At 5th, 7th, 9th, and 11th level, you may learn two additional techniques from the list. When you gain a level in this class, you may exchange a technique you know for a different one from the list.

### Total Concentration Breathing
You have learned to expand your lungs as you breathe, circulating air through your body to improve your physical capabilities. As a bonus action, you may enter this state to gain one of the following benefits for 1 minute:
- **Improved Stamina.** You gain temporary hit points equal to twice your level.
- **Improved Speed.** Your movement speed increases by 10 ft.
- **Improved Strength.** You gain advantage on Strength checks and saving throws.
If you use this feature again, you may gain a different benefit and refresh the duration of any benefits you already have. You may use this feature a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a short or long rest.

### Slayer’s Mark
There is a deep power that rests within you, only awakening through intense, near-death experiences. At 3rd level, when you are brought below 0 hit points by an enemy intending to kill, roll a d100. If you roll equal to or below twice your ranger level, a mark manifests on your skin, and you stand back up with 1 hit point, granting you a surge of power:
- You gain 1d10 + your twice your level temporary hit points.
- You have advantage on all melee weapon attack rolls.
- Once per turn, if you have no spell slots remaining, you may cast a Breathing Technique at one level below your highest spell slot level (minimum 1).
The mark’s power is fleeting. At the end of your turn, roll a 1d4. On a 1 or a 2, the mark disappears, you lose all of its gained benefits, and immediately gain a level of exhaustion. The mark also disappears if 1 minute passes, you are incapacitated, or you die.

<div class="break"></div>

The more you gain this mark through near-death experiences, the more control you have over it. After the 3rd time you acquire this mark through a roll, the die to end the effect becomes a d6, and you may awaken the mark as an action. You may use this ability once per long rest.

### Recovery Breathing
Starting at 7th level, by focusing your mind and drawing slow, deliberate breaths, you can rapidly speed up the healing and stamina recovery process of your body. As an action, you may roll a number of Hit Die equal to your proficiency bonus and regain that many hit points. Also, you immediately regain a number of spell slots with a level sum equal to half your proficiency bonus.

<div class="break"></div>

This feature can be used once per long rest.

### Improved Total Concentration Breathing
At 7th level, when your Total Concentration Breathing is active, you can replace one of your weapon attacks with the casting of a Breathing Technique that has a casting time of 1 action.

### Total Concentration Breathing: Constant
Beginning at 11th level, you have learned to always maintain your Total Concentration Breathing, allowing you to hold on to its benefits even when you are asleep. At the end of a long rest, choose one of the benefits of Total Concentration Breathing to maintain. You gain this benefit until you change it at the end of a different long rest or you fall below 0 hit points.

### Permanent Slayer Mark
At 15th level, your slayer’s mark appears on your skin and remains there permanently. While you do not gain the usual effects of the mark unless it is awakened, you do gain the following benefits:
- Your movement speed increases by 10 ft.
- Your hit point maximum increases by 1 for each level in this class.
- You gain advantage in Dexterity (Acrobatics) checks.
You only gain the original benefits when the mark is awakened through the once per long rest ability or through rolling.
