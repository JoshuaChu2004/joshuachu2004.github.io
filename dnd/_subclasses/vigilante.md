***Within every society, there are those who disturb the quiet, seek to harm others. Criminals, politicians, religious zealots, injustice is found around every corner. Vigilantes make it their mission to bring down these lowlifes, utilizing their vast array of skills to overcome the odds. Depending on their reputation, their name may be shouted and cheered in the taverns, or spoken only in hushed whispers out of fear.***

| Rogue Level | Features |
|------------|--------|
| 3rd        | Reputation, Modus Operandi |
| 9th        | Renown |
| 13th       | Information Network |
| 17th       | Symbol |

### Reputation
At 3rd level, your actions have attracted the attention of political bodies and the general public, allowing their opinions of you to affect your methods. Your Reputation’s state can be one of three: Fear, Justice, and Hope, which augment your Sneak Attack.  

Many future features will force creatures to make saving throws and refer to your **Reputation Save DC**, which is calculated as follows: 8 + your proficiency + your Charisma modifier. 

- **Fear.** When you land your Sneak Attack, you may roll one less Sneak Attack die to force the target to make a Wisdom saving throw. On a fail, they are Frightened until the end of your next turn. Also, creatures that are Frightened of you have disadvantage on saving throws against your Reputation Save DC.
- **Justice.** When you land your Sneak Attack, you may roll one less Sneak Attack die to force the target to make a Strength saving throw. On a fail, they have disadvantage on attack rolls until the end of your next turn. Also, creatures that have disadvantage on attacks have disadvantage on saving throws against your Reputation Save DC.
- **Hope.** When you land your Sneak Attack, you may roll one less Sneak Attack die to grant an ally (not including yourself) within 30 ft. that can see or hear you to gain 1d6 + your proficiency bonus Temporary Hit Points until the end of your next turn. Also, hostile creatures within 10 ft. of an ally have disadvantage on saving throws against your Reputation Save DC.

You should work with your DM to determine what state your Reputation is currently in. Your Reputation may change during an adventure, depending on the actions you take, though it can often take a significant event or a long time to do so.

### Modus Operandi
At 3rd level, you also form a pattern of how you operate and behave.  

**Operandi.** You know three Operandi. Only one Operandi may be applied to the same action. You learn an additional Operandi at 5th level (4), 9th level (5), 13th level (6), and 17th level (7). When you gain a level in this class, you may replace an Operandi you know with a different one from the list.  

**Modus Points.** To use an Operandi, you must spend a Modus point to do so. You have a number of Modus points equal to the number of Sneak Attack dice you have (minimum 3), which are regained at the end of a short or long rest.

### Renown
At 9th level, your reputation precedes you. Criminals, guards, and politicians within your region have more than heard of you, the mere suggestion of your presence will provoke a reaction from them. Depending on your reputation state, you gain a new Cunning Action.
- **Intimidate (Fear).** Pressure a creature within 30 ft. with your reputation, forcing them to make a Wisdom saving throw. On a fail, they are Frightened until the end of your next turn.
- **Disarm (Justice).** Attempt to snatch a creature’s weapon away from them. The creature must be within Melee range. Make a Sleight of Hand check contested by their Athletics or Acrobatics check. On a success, you force them to drop a weapon they are holding. If you have a free hand, you may choose to catch it. 
- **Inspire (Hope).** Bolster the morale of your allies, granting one within 30 ft. that can see or hear you advantage on their next attack roll or saving throw until the end of their next turn.

### Punishing Strike
Also at 9th level, you are able to capitalize on your reputation on those affected by it. When you land your Sneak Attack on a creature, you may roll an additional Sneak Attack die if the creature has disadvantage on saving throws against your Reputation DC.

### Information Network
At 13th level, you have garnered a proper information network nearly everywhere you go to aid you on your crusade. Determine with your DM the nature of your network along with the scale and spread of its reach. While you have access to your information network, you can cast the Clairvoyance spell without material components, however, the target of the spell can only be a location you have been in already. Charisma is your spellcasting modifier for this spell. Once cast, you can not do so again until the end of a long rest. Also, you can spend 1 hour in a populated area to investigate, interrogate, and scout the inner workings of the region. The DM answers two of the following questions, based on the knowledge and opinions of local people:
- Who has control over the localized area?
- Who do the people fear in this area?
- Where is the nearest hideout for criminals or other groups of people?
- What do people think of local law and government?
- What is the latest gossip around the area?
- What are people’s thoughts on a certain event, organization, or person?
- Is there anywhere in the surrounding area considered “unsafe”?

Once this part of the feature is used, you can not do so again until after a long rest.

### Symbol
At 17th level, you have become a symbol of what you represent, able to spur reactions from everyone who has heard of you. When you roll initiative, you may cause one of the following effects depending on your reputation state:
- **Symbol of Fear (Fear).** Evoke dread and despair against those that stand in your way. Choose up to five creatures within 60 ft. that can see you to make a Wisdom saving throw. Upon a fail, they are frightened for 1 minute. They may reroll the saving throw at the end of its turn.
- **Symbol of Justice (Justice).** Display unyielding determination and wavers the resolve of those that threaten others. All creatures that can see you of your choice within 60 ft. are forced to make a Charisma saving throw. Upon a fail, they have disadvantage on attacks until the end of your next turn.
- **Symbol of Hope (Hope).** Uplift those around you with your presence. All friendly creatures within 20 ft. gain advantage on their next ability check, attack roll, or saving throw. For 1 minute, while a friendly creature is within 20 ft. of you, they may use their reaction to add your Charisma modifier to an ability check, attack roll, or saving throw.
