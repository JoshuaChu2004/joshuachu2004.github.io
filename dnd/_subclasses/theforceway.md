***Monks of the Way of the Force have gained telekinetic abilities and can channel the ki of objects and opponents without touching them. Their abilities grow and expand as they get stronger, as well as morph to fit their personal psyche. They are pacifists, peacekeepers, roamers, and seekers of vengeance.***

| Monk Level | Features |
|------------|--------|
| 3rd        | Force Techniques, Force Perception |
| 6th        | Force Alignment, Enhanced Telekinesis |
| 11th       | Meditation |
| 17th       | Force Projection |

### Force Techniques
Starting when you choose this tradition at 3rd level, you can manipulate the ki of objects and opponents without touching them. You may choose two techniques. When you gain a level in Monk, you may swap out one technique for another. 
<div class="break"> </div>
As you gain levels, you will gain access to new techniques and may choose two additional techniques at 6th, 11th, and 17th level.

### Force Perception
Also at 3rd level, your natural attunement with the Force grants you the ability to feel any ripples through it, sensing emotions around you and reacting to danger before it even happens. You gain a bonus to your Passive Insight equal to your proficiency bonus. 
<div class="break"> </div>
When you roll initiative, you may spend at least 1 ki point to add a d8 to your roll. For every two ki points you spend in total, you gain a +1 bonus to your AC and saving throws for 1 minute, to a maximum bonus equal to your proficiency bonus.
<div class="break"> </div>

You may also receive visions, whispers, or stronger ripples in the Force that serve as vague premonitions for the future or reveal unknown information. These messages often occur in areas strong in the Force or when a very influential event sends a ripple outwards.

### Force Alignment
At 6th level, your attunement to the Force has expanded, as your abilities begin to be molded by your own personality and morals. Your standing with the force is as follows, depending on your alignment:
#### Light (Good)
If your alignment is Good, then you are a Light side Force user. You place an importance on life, compassion, and enlightenment. You process through your emotions and let them go to follow the will of the Force. You are able to utilize Light and Neutral side Force techniques.

#### Neutral (Neutral)
If your alignment is Neutral, then you are a Neutral side Force user. You focus on flexibility and balance, recognizing that neither the Light or the Dark side can exist without the other. You follow your own path without allowing your emotions or stoicism to blind you. You can utilize Light, Neutral, and Dark Force techniques, but the number of Light and Dark force Techniques can never differ by more than 1.

#### Dark (Evil)
If your alignment is Evil, then you are a Dark side Force user. Power, domination, and control are the tenets you choose to live by. Your passion, hatred, and emotions fuel your abilities, leading to incredible amounts of influence on the Force, for it is yours to command. You are able to utilize Dark and Neutral Force techniques.

<div class="break"> </div>

Future features granted by this tradition are influenced by your standing with the Force. Just as your alignment can change over the course of an adventure, so can your standing with the Force. If you wish to choose a different standing with the Force than the one your alignment assigns for you, you should speak with your DM to determine a good way to work that into the world they have created.

### Enhanced Telekinesis
Also at 6th level, you have enough practice with the basics of Force powers to apply additional effects to creatures that are affected by them.
#### Neutralizing Push (Light)
When a creature fails a saving throw and is moved by your Force Techniques, they have disadvantage on attacks until the end of their next turn.
#### Debilitating Push (Neutral)	
When a creature fails a saving throw and is moved by your Force Techniques, their movement is halved for their next turn and they have disadvantage on Dexterity saving throws.
#### Bruising Push (Dark)
When a creature fails a saving throw and is moved by your Force Techniques, they take force damage equal to your Wisdom modifier.
All future features will differ based on your standing.

### Meditation
Beginning at 11th level, you can focus your mind on your values and explore your own thoughts, granting you a deeper understanding of the Force and your abilities. As an action, enter a meditative state to recover and focus your abilities. While meditating, you gain a bonus to your AC and saving throws equal to your proficiency bonus. At the start of your next turn, you exit your trance, gaining one of the following states for 10 minutes:
#### Stoic (Light)
You reflect on the values of letting go of your emotions, steeling your body from outside influences and seeing through the emotions of others. You have advantage on Insight checks, are immune to being Charmed and Frightened, and your bonus from Force Perception is doubled.
If you were undisturbed during your meditation, suffering no damage or conditions, you may regain ki point once per turn when you take damage.
#### Vigilant (Neutral)
You pay close attention to everything around you, for ki flows through everything, living or dead. You have advantage on Perception checks, you cannot be surprised and creatures can not be Hidden from you, and you may spend an additional ki point after using Deflect Attack to restore your reaction.
If you were undisturbed during your meditation, suffering no damage or conditions, you may regain a ki point once per turn when a creature misses an attack against you.
#### Ruthless (Dark)
You plunge yourself into your passions and hatred, allowing your emotions to bubble and churn. You have advantage on Intimidation checks, you are immune to being Prone or Restrained, and Force techniques cost one less ki point (minimum of 1).
If you were undisturbed during your meditation, suffering no damage or conditions, you may regain a ki point once per turn when you succeed an attack against another creature.

### Force Projection
At 17th level, you gain the ability to create an almost lifelike image of yourself which has behaviors depending on your standing with the Force.

#### Force Ghost (Light)
As a reaction to taking damage that would knock you unconscious, you may use 5 ki points to summon a ghost-like image of yourself. Your ghost uses your game statistics, though it only has 50 hit points. It shares your initiative and on its turn, you can control it to move around, use the Dodge, Dash, and Disengage actions, or utilize your Force techniques. Your ghost disappears when you regain conciousness, though each ki point you use through your ghost counts as two ki points.
#### Force Mirage (Neutral)
As a bonus action, you can use 5 ki points to summon your mirage that follows and mirrors your exact movements. While your Mirage is active, all attacks against you have disadvantage as enemies must guess which is the real you. As a reaction to a creature missing an attack, you may use a Force technique.
#### Force Phantom (Dark)
As an action, you can use 5 ki points to summon a dark, silhouetted image of yourself in an unoccupied space within 15 ft. of you. While your phantom does not take turns, it uses your game statistics, though it only has 30 hit points. Once on your turn, your phantom may move up to your movement speed. Whenever you succeed an attack, you may use a Force technique through your phantom without using any actions.
