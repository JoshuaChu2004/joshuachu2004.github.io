***Every good lead needs a supporting act. The comedic relief to the straight man. Bards of the College of Jesters are widely renowned for their ability to bring up those around them. Whether it be setting their mates for a punchline or a knife through the chest. Alone, they may not be quite so fearsome, but amongst their merry band of compatriots, they will bring out the best.***

| Bard Level | Features |
|------------|----------|
| 3rd        | Encore, Supporting Act |
| 6th   | Master of Inspiration |
| 6th   | Master of Inspiration |
| 14th  | Finale |


### Encore
With your inspiring grace, you can bow out and grant an ally the spotlight of the stage in your stead. As an action, choose a creature you can see within 30 ft. That creature can then immediately use its reaction to move up to half its movement speed and take an action or bonus action. 
Once per short rest, if the creature’s reaction triggers an effect described in Supporting Act, you regain a Bardic Inspiration die.

You can use this feature a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus. You regain any expended uses at the end of a long rest.

### Supporting Act
Once per round when you cast a spell or inflict a condition that targets a creature, you may expend a use of your Bardic Inspiration to prepare them for your ally's next move. You roll your Bardic Inspiration die for the following effects occur when an appropriate trigger is met:
- If an attack against the creature misses, the attack roll is increased by the number rolled + your Charisma modifier.
- If the creature is hit by an attack, the attack deals additional damage equal to the number rolled + your Charisma modifier.
- If the creature makes a saving throw, their roll is subtracted by the number rolled.

If no effect is triggered after 1 minute, the condition ends. Once per short rest, you may regain a Bardic Inspiration die when the condition ends in this way.

### Master of Inspiration
Starting at 6th level, when you roll a Bardic Inspiration die that does not affect you, you may choose to reroll the die, always taking the new result.

### Finale
Starting at 14th level, when a creature affected by Supporting Act is targeted by an attack or damaging spell from an ally, you may choose to ignore any triggers for the feature. This must be done before the outcome of the attack or spell is known.  If the creature is hit or fails the saving throw, they take maximum damage instead of rolling.
