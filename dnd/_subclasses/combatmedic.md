***Combat Medics are a true sight for sore eyes on the battlefield. When comrades and enemies lie on the ground wounded, the Combat Medics are the ones in war aiming to save lives rather than take them. Wielding bandages, needles, and herbs, they will do their utmost to make sure their allies stay in the fight and more importantly, stay alive.***

| Fighter Level | Features |
|----------------|--------|
| 3rd            | Emergency Surgery, Trained Healer |
| 7th            | Rapid Procedure, Resource Management |
| 10th           | Shock Therapy |
| 15th           | Invigorating Surgery |
| 18th           | Angel of the Wounded |

### Emergency Surgery
At 3rd level, you can use a Healer’s Kit as a bonus action to perform rapid stitching and apply pain killing herbs to a creature within 5 ft. with less than half of its hit points remaining. They gain 1d10 + half your fighter level (rounded down) temporary hit points for 1 minute.
<div class="break"> </div>
You may use this feature a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus. You regain expended uses at the end of a short rest.

### Trained Healer
Also at 3rd level, you gain proficiency in the Wisdom (Medicine) skill. You double your proficiency bonus added to those checks. You can create a Healer’s Kit in 1 hour, costing seven gold pieces as supplies.

### Rapid Procedure
At 7th level, your skilled hands have made you even quicker to perform life saving maneuvers. Any actions that use a Healer’s Kit may now be done in place of a weapon attack.

### Resource Management
Also at 7th level, you have become excellent at maintaining your tools for as long as possible. Healer’s Kits start out with twenty uses for you instead of ten. When another creature uses your Healer’s Kit, they expend two uses instead of one.

### Shock Therapy
At 10th level, you have learned that sometimes your allies need a painful shock to get back into the fight. If you damage an allied creature within 5 ft. that is afflicted with the Charmed, Dazed, Frightened, Incapacitated, or Stunned, you may expend a use of a Healer’s Kit to end the condition.
<div class="break"> </div>
You may use this feature once per short rest.

### Invigorating Surgery
At 15th level, the tools and ingredients that make up your Healer’s Kit are also laced with empowering materials. When you use a Healer’s Kit on a creature, they gain advantage on their next ability check, attack roll, or saving throw.

### Angel of the Wounded
At 18th level, the mere sight of you serves as a shining beacon to your comrades. When you use a Healer’s Kit on a creature, you may expend an additional use to grant up to seven creatures within 30 ft. of your choice to gain 1d10 + your fighter level temporary hit points.
<div class="break"> </div>
You may use this feature once per short rest.
