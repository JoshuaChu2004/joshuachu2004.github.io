# School of the Bear
***Witchers of the School of the Bear are known for being loners, seeking out little personal contact except for what is necessary on the Path. They think of themselves not as a race or group, but simply a profession. Because of this, there is often little comradery amongst Bear Witchers. Strong, large, and durable, they are the Witchers that have pushed their physical capabilities the furthest.***

| Witcher Level | Features |
|---------------|----------|
| 3rd           | Ursine Discipline |
| 7th           | Hardened Brews |
| 11th          | Steadfast |
| 15th          | Unstoppable |
| 18th          | Path of the Bear |

### Ursine Discipline
At 3rd level, the School of the Bear has trained you to be a monument to physical capability. You have advantage on Strength (Athletics) checks and when you miss a melee attack, you may expend an Adrenaline point to make a Grapple or Shove action as a minor action. Also, your hit point maximum increases by 1 for each level in Witcher you have.

### Hardened Brews
At 7th level, you have learned to imbue your potions with herbs that harden your body from harm. For each potion you have imbibed at once, you may reduce all damage except for Psychic and Force by 1 (to a minimum of 1).
Once per round, you gain an Adrenaline point when you take damage.

### Steadfast
At 11th level, you have learned to remain unwavered in your defenses. When you use Reactive Slip against an attack, you only take quarter damage. Also, your next attack against that creature that hits until the end of your next turn deals an additional d8 of damage.

### Unstoppable
At 15th level, even when you are weak, when your body is all but broken, you muster up the strength to continue fighting. When you fall below 0 hit points, you may consume all Adrenaline points to regain a d10 + your Constitution modifier hit points for each Adrenaline point spent.
<div class="break"></div>
You may use this feature once per long rest.

### Path of the Bear
At 18th level, you are a master of your school’s teachings, being able to endure the worst conditions the world has to offer. On top of the damage reduction from Hardened Brews, you reduce all damage taken by your Constitution modifier. Also, when you roll Initiative, you automatically cast Quen on yourself without costing an action or Adrenaline point.
