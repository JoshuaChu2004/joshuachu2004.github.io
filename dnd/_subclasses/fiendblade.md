***You or your ancestor before had an intense and dangerous encounter with a creature from the Plane of Fire that left you cursed to wield its volatile flames. While most would be unable to control their powers, wreaking havoc wherever they go, you have managed to harness your fire by channeling it through the metal of weapons.***

| Sorcerer Level | Features |
|------------|--------|
| 1st        | Fiendish Blood, Forged in Fire |
| 6th        | Extra Attack, Burning Blood |
| 14th       | Fiendish Charm |
| 18th       | Firestorm |

### Fiendish Blood
You learn additional spells when you reach certain levels in this class, as shown on the Fiend Spells table. Each of these spells counts as a sorcerer spell for you, but it doesn’t count against the number of sorcerer spells you know.

### Forged in Fire
As a result of your hellish blood, you can bond with a weapon by spilling your blood on a weapon that lacks the heavy property and meditating with it for 1 hour. While bonded, you are proficient with the weapon and can recall it back into your hand as an action as long as it is within 1 mile of you. Furthermore, while the weapon is being carried or held, you gain the following benefits:
- You gain a bonus to your AC equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum of +1) as long as you are not wearing armor or donning a shield.
- You have resistance to fire damage.
- When a creature takes fire damage from your spells, they are set alight, taking fire damage equal to your Charisma modifier at the start of their turn for 1 minute. They can be put out as an action, being doused in water, or by taking cold damage. If the spell has a saving throw and the creature succeeds, they are not set aflame.
- When a creature set alight by your spells goes below 0 hit points, you regain 1d6 + your Charisma modifier hit points.

### Extra Attack
Starting at 6th level, you can attack twice, instead of once, whenever you take the Attack action on your turn. Moreover, you can cast one of your cantrips in place of one of those attacks.

### Burning Blood
Also at 6th level, you can now consume nearby fires to fortify your body. As a bonus action, you may extinguish a number of creatures equal to your proficiency bonus within 30 ft. of you. You then gain a d4 of Temporary Hit Points for each creature extinguished.
You can use this feature a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus. You regain all uses at the end of a long rest.

### Fiendish Charm
At 14th level, you gain the ability to weave words together like a dealing devil. You can spend 2 sorcery points to gain advantage on a Charisma check. You may use this after rolling, though before the result is known.

### Firestorm
Beginning at 18th level, you can stoke the fires in your blood to temporarily amplify your Fire magic. As an action, you can spend 5 sorcery points to start a Firestorm for 1 minute. While Firestorm is active, you may choose a creature within 30 ft. to set on fire at the start of your turn.
