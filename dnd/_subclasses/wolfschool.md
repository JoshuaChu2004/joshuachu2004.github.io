***Witchers of the School of the Wolf have carved out their reputations as well-balanced, esteemed monster hunters. True generalists, their school’s philosophy lies closest to the original Order of Witchers, which is also reflected in their training as they do not often specialize in one discipline or another. Though their widespread skills often make them versatile and formidable foes.***


| 3rd     | Wolven Discipline       |
| 7th     | Potions and Mutations   |
| 11th    | Retaliation   |
| 15th    | Crowd Control |
| 18th    | Path of the Wolf        |

### Wolven Discipline

At 3rd level, the School of the Wolf has trained you to favor consistency and utilize a wide range of skills to overcome your opponents. While in combat, your maximum Adrenaline points are increased by two. Also, when you make a weapon attack, you may spend an Adrenaline point to grant yourself advantage on the To Hit roll.

### Potions and Mutations

At 7th level, you have delved further into the traditional practices of being a witcher, allowing you to brew an additional recipe when you finish a short or long rest. You also choose an additional Mutagen.
Once per round, you gain an Adrenaline point when you drink a potion or apply an oil.

### Retaliation

At 11th level, your body automatically reacts to pain. When you use Reactive Slip and reduce the damage to below your Constitution Score, you may also make a melee weapon attack against the target if they are within range.

### Crowd Control

At 15th level, the rapid flow between sword swings and Signs allow you to survive even when being swarmed. After succeeding a weapon attack against a creature, you may spend an additional Adrenaline point to cast a Sign without using a weapon attack. This may only be used once per round.

### Path of the Wolf
At 18th level, you have truly mastered the philosophy and techniques of the School of the Wolf, possibly surpassing even your mentors before you. You now gain an Adrenaline point for every weapon attack you succeed and once per round, you may spend an Adrenaline point to roll an additional damage die on a weapon attack.
