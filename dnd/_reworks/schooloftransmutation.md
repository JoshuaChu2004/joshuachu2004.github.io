***You are a student of spells that modify energy and matter. To you, the world is not a fixed thing, but eminently mutable, and you delight in being an agent of change. You wield the raw stuff of creation and learn to alter both physical forms and mental qualities. Your magic gives you the tools to become a smith on reality's forge.***

<div class="break"></div>

***Some transmuters are tinkerers and pranksters, turning people into toads and transforming copper into silver for fun and occasional profit. Others pursue their magical studies with deadly seriousness, seeking the power of the gods to make and destroy worlds.***

| Wizard Level | Features |
|--------------|----------|
| 2nd          | Transmutation Savant, Transmuter’s Stone |
| 6th          | Minor Alchemy |
| 10th         | Shapechanger |
| 14th         | Master Transmuter |

## Transmutation Savant
Beginning when you select this school at 2nd level, the gold and time you must spend to copy a Transmutation spell into your spellbook is halved.

## Transmuter's Stone
Starting at 2nd level when you select this school, you can spend 8 hours creating a transmuter's stone that stores transmutation magic. You can benefit from the stone yourself or give it to another creature. A creature gains a benefit of your choice as long as the stone is in the creature's possession. When you create the stone, choose the benefit from the following options:
- Darkvision out to a range of 60 feet
- An increase to speed of 10 feet while the creature is unencumbered
- Proficiency in Constitution saving throws
- Resistance to acid, cold, fire, lightning, or thunder damage (your choice whenever you choose this benefit)
- Immunity to fall damage as the creature floats safely down
- Advantage on an ability check (once per day)

Each time you cast a transmutation spell of 1st level or higher, you can change the effect of your stone if the stone is on your person.

<div class="break"></div>

If you create a new transmuter's stone, the previous one ceases to function.

## Minor Alchemy
Starting at 6th level, you can temporarily alter the physical properties of one nonmagical object, changing it from one substance into another. As an action or reaction to an object moving or being a visible threat to you or someone else within 30 ft., you perform a special alchemical procedure on one object that is not being worn or carried by another creature, transmutating the material to another base material. The material must be simple, such as a single element, or a common compound such as steel or paper. You must know the material with intimate detail to use it for this feature and you can not change a material’s temperature or state of matter. If used as an action or reaction, the material remains altered until the end of your next turn. You can extend the casting of the procedure to 10 minutes, causing the material to maintain its new form for up to 1 hour.

<div class="break"></div>

You can use this feature three times per long rest. If you perform the procedure over 10 minutes it does not consume a use.

## Shapechanger
At 10th level, you add the Polymorph spell to your spellbook, if it is not there already. You can cast Polymorph without expending a spell slot. When you do so, you can target only yourself.

<div class="break"></div>

Once you cast Polymorph in this way, you can't do so again until you finish a short or long rest, though you can still cast it normally using an available spell slot.

## Master Transmuter
Starting at 14th level, you can use your action to consume the reserve of transmutation magic stored within your transmuter's stone in a single burst. When you do so, choose one of the following effects. Your transmuter's stone is destroyed and can't be remade until you finish a long rest.

<div class="break"></div>

***Major Transformation.*** You can transmute one nonmagical object – no larger than a 5-foot cube – into another nonmagical object of similar size and mass and of equal or lesser value. You must spend 10 minutes handling the object to transform it.  

***Panacea.*** You remove all curses, diseases, and poisons affecting a creature that you touch with the transmuter's stone. The creature also regains all its hit points.  

***Restore Life.*** You cast the Raise Dead spell on a creature you touch with the transmuter's stone, without expending a spell slot or needing to have the spell in your spellbook.  

***Restore Youth.*** You touch the transmuter's stone to a willing creature, and that creature's apparent age is reduced by 3d10 years, to a minimum of 13 years. This effect doesn't extend the creature's lifespan.
