A creature you touch regains a number of hit points equal to 1d8 + your spellcasting ability modifier. If the creature has more than 0 hit points, it may roll a Hit Die and add it to the amount healed. This spell has no effect on undead or constructs.

<div class="break"></div>

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, the healing increases by 1d8 and the creature may roll an additional Hit Die for each slot level above 1st.
