You choose a creature you can see within range and mystically mark it as your quarry. You may also target a creature’s tracks or traces it may leave behind. Until the spell ends, you have advantage on Wisdom (Perception) and Wisdom (Survival) checks made to find the creature. You are able to cast other spells that require Concentration without ending this one, though you lose the effects of this spell until it is the only one you are concentrating on. Choose one of the following effects to apply to the creature:
- **Marked for Death.** You deal an extra 1d6 damage to the target whenever you hit it with a weapon attack.
- **Eagle Vision.** You are always aware of the creature’s location as long as you are within 100 ft. of them, even if you can not see them. The target can avoid this if they are protected from divination magic or have a similar effect.
- **Unsettling Mark.** Your mark hampers their defenses. When your marked creature makes a saving throw and succeeds, you may decrease their roll by a d6 as a reaction.
- **Mark of Hinderance.** When you succeed a weapon attack against the creature, their speed is reduced by 10 ft. until the end of your next turn (to a minimum of 10).
- **Precise Strike.** You add a d6 to your attack rolls against the target.  
As a bonus action, you can change the effect applied to a creature. Also, if the target drops to 0 hit points before the spell ends, you can use a bonus action on subsequent turns to mark a new creature.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2rd or 3rd level, you can maintain your concentration on the spell for up to 8 hours. When you use a spell slot of 4th level or higher, you can maintain your concentration on the spell for up to 24 hours.
