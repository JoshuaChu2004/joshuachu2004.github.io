**Requirements:** To make most potions or chemical mixtures, you must have proficiency in Alchemist's Tools or an Herbalism Kit. You must also have the required potion supplies to brew a potion. Each rarity of potion has a different cost, shown as a GP amount worth of supplies required. At most herbalists or sometimes even general stores, you can purchase potion supplies directly with GP. Likewise, you may also forage in certain places to acquire supplies. Each potion rarity has an established DC, which you will roll against throughout the brewing.

<div class="break"></div>

**Process:** You must have an actual Herbalism Kit or Alchemist's Tools to begin brewing a potion, with the required supply cost immediately on hand. Once the brewing has started, the supplies are consumed and you must make a Arcana check (with Alchemist Supplies) or a Nature Check (with Herbalism Kit), adding your proficiency to the roll (doubled if expertise), against the rarity's DC. If someone else with proficiency in Nature, Arcana, Alchemists Tools, or Herbalism Kit is helping you, you may roll with advantage. Once begun, you will have to roll this same check several more times before the brewing has completed. When the potion is completed, if you rolled more failures than successes, then the potion becomes unusable.

<div class="break"></div>

**Time:** You cannot pause the progress of making a potion and resume it at a later time. Each rarity specifies how many times you must tend to a potion (spread evenly throughout the brewing) and for how long you must spend for the interaction to count. Each time you tend to the potion, you must make another check against the potion's DC.

- **Common:** 12 Hours (1, 1 hr)
- **Uncommon:** 3 days (3, 2 hrs)
- **Rare:** 7 days (7, 2 hrs)
- **Very Rare:** 3 weeks (21, 3 hrs)
- **Legendary:** 6 months (24, 4 hrs)

**Cost:** Every potion requires some amount of supplies, with its rarity corresponding to the cost.

- **Common:** 5 GP
- **Uncommon:** 10 GP
- **Rare:** 40 GP
- **Very Rare:** 120 GP
- **Legendary:** 500 GP

**DC:** Every potion has a DC that you must roll a Arcana check (with Alchemist Supplies) or a Nature Check (with Herbalism Kit), adding your proficiency to the roll (doubled if expertise), against.

- **Common:** DC 8
- **Uncommon:** DC 10
- **Rare:** DC 13
- **Very Rare:** DC 16
- **Legendary:** DC 20

**Learning how to make a potion:** Every potion has some amount of studying that needs to be done in order to create it, even if you are copying instructions from a written source.

- **Common:** 2 hours
- **Uncommon:** 8 hours
- **Rare:** 24 hours
- **Very Rare:** 50 hours
- **Legendary:** 120 hours
