title: Bleeding Condition
type: combat
finished: false
---
A bleeding creature takes damage equal to its proficiency bonus at the start of its turn. It also has disadvantage on Strength, Dexterity, and Constitution ability checks and saving throws. It is removed by regaining hit points or by a creature making a successful Medicine check (DC 10 or half the damage taken, whichever is higher) as an action.
