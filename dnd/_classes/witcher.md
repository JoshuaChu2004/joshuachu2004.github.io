A witcher is a skilled fighter who uses their magic to enhance their combat abilities.

## Class Features

### Hit Points
- **Hit Dice:** 1d10 per witcher level
- **Hit Points at 1st Level:** 10 + your Constitution modifier
- **Hit Points at Higher Levels:** 1d10 (or 6) + your Constitution modifier per witcher level after 1st

### Proficiencies
- **Armor:** Light armor, medium armor
- **Weapons:** Simple weapons, martial weapons
- **Tools:** None
- **Saving Throws:** Wisdom, Charisma, Dexterity
- **Skills:** Choose two from Athletics, Insight, Intimidation, Medicine, Persuasion, and Religion

## Divine Strike

Starting at 8th level, you gain the ability to infuse your weapon strikes with magical energy. Once on each of your turns when you hit a creature with a weapon attack, you can cause the attack to deal an extra 1d8 radiant damage to the target.

## Channel Divinity

You gain the ability to channel magical energy directly from your deity, using that energy to fuel magical effects. You start with two such effects: Turn Undead and an effect determined by your domain.

### Turn Undead

As an action, you present your holy symbol and speak a prayer censuring the undead. Each undead that can see or hear you within 30 feet of you must make a Wisdom saving throw. If the creature fails its saving throw, it is turned for 1 minute or until it takes damage. If the creature is turned, it is immune to your Turn Undead for the duration.

## Divine Domain

Choose one domain related to your deity: War, Life, or Tempest. Your choice grants you domain spells and other features when you choose it at 1st level. You can choose a different domain at 6th and 17th level.

### War Domain

                The War domain represents the magical power of war and conquest. Witchers of this domain are fierce warriors who use their magical energy to enhance their martial prowess.

**Domain Spells:** You gain domain spells at the witcher levels noted in the War Domain Spells table.
