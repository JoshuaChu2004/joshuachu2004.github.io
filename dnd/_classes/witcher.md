***Normal individuals turned freaks of nature by the horrendous experiments known as the Trial of Grasses, Witchers are professional monster hunters who traditionally seek out contracts for coin. They are trained at a young age to be expert swordsmen, to memorize alchemical recipes and bestiarys, and to cast minor spells known as Signs to overcome their foes. Some beings despise their existence, many fear the twin blades on their back, but all cry for a Witcher when a monster threatens their lives.***

*You must have a Dexterity score or Strength score and a Wisdom score of 13 or higher in order to multiclass in or out of this class.*

## Class Features

As a Witcher, you gain the following class features.

### Hit Points

**Hit Dice:** 1d10 per Witcher level

**Hit Points at 1st Level:** 10 + your Constitution modifier

**Hit Points at Higher Levels:** 1d10 (or 6) + your Constitution modifier per Witcher level after 1st

### Proficiencies

**Armor:** Light armor, medium armor, heavy armor, shields

**Weapons:** Simple weapons, martial weapons

**Tools:** Alchemist’s Supplies

**Saving Throws:** Dexterity, Wisdom

**Skills:** Choose three from Acrobatics, Arcana, Athletics, History, Insight, Intimidation, Investigation, Medicine, Nature, Perception, Survival

### Equipment

You start with the following equipment, in addition to the equipment granted by your background:

- (a) scale mail or (b) leather armor
- (a) a steel longsword
  (a) a silver longsword or (b) a silvered martial weapon
- (a) a dungeoneer's pack or (b) an explorer's pack
- A dagger
- Alchemist’s supplies

### Mutations

Beginning at 1st level, you have undergone the horrific Trial of the Grasses, which has irrevocably altered your body. You gain the following benefits:

- Your pupils have changed to look like that of a cat, granting you darkvision, up to a range of 30 ft.
- You have advantage on Wisdom (Perception) and Wisdom (survival) checks based on sight or smell.
- You may add your proficiency bonus to Initiative rolls.
- You are immune to most diseases.
- The trials have rendered you sterile, preventing you from having children.
- You age dramatically slower, extending your lifespan by up to 5 times.

At 9th level, your mutations develop further, granting you the following additional benefits:

- Your darkvision is increased to a range of 60 ft.
- Your walking speed increases by 10 ft.
- Your blindsight range is increased by 10 ft.

### Alchemy

You have spent many hours studying various alchemical mixes to create potions and oils to aid you on the path. At 1st level, you know three recipes from the list found at the end of the class description. At the end of a short or long rest, you may brew a number of concoctions equal to your proficiency bonus with Alchemist’s supplies. Some brews could be from the same recipe, though you can only carry 2 uses of a certain recipe at once, and only Witchers can drink the potions you create.

<div class="break"></div>
Despite your mutations allowing you to consume your potions, they are still dangerous in large quantities. 
- **If you have two Witcher potions active at the same time:** You begin taking 1d6 + your proficiency bonus poison damage at the start of your turn until both potion effects wear off. 
- **If you have three Witcher potions active at the same time:** You are Poisoned, take 1d10 + your proficiency bonus poison damage at the start of your turn, and healing is halved until all potion effects wear off. At this stage, you can not drink another potion.

When you level up, you may choose an additional brew to learn from the list or you may enhance an existing brew you know.

### Fighting Style

You adopt a particular style of fighting as your specialty. Choose one of the following options. You can't take a Fighting Style option more than once, even if you later get to choose again.

- **Blind Fighting (TCE).** You have blindsight with a range of 10 feet. Within that range, you can effectively see anything that isn't behind total cover, even if you're blinded or in darkness. Moreover, you can see an invisible creature within that range, unless the creature successfully hides from you.
- **Defense (PHB).** While you are wearing armor, you gain a +1 bonus to AC.
- **Dueling (PHB).** When you are wielding a melee weapon in one hand and no other weapons, you gain a +2 bonus to damage rolls with that weapon.
- **Great Weapon Fighting (PHB).** When you roll a 1 or 2 on a damage die for an attack you make with a melee weapon that you are wielding with two hands, you can reroll the die and must use the new roll, even if the new roll is a 1 or a 2. The weapon must have the two-handed or versatile property for you to gain this benefit.
- **Interception (TCE).** When a creature you can see hits a target, other than you, within 5 feet of you with an attack, you can use your reaction to reduce the damage the target takes by 1d10 + your proficiency bonus (to a minimum of 0 damage). You must be wielding a shield or a simple or martial weapon to use this reaction.
- **Protection (PHB).** When a creature you can see attacks a target other than you that is within 5 feet of you, you can use your reaction to impose disadvantage on the attack roll. You must be wielding a shield.
- **Superior Technique (TCE).** You learn one maneuver of your choice from among those available to the Battle Master archetype. If a maneuver you use requires your target to make a saving throw to resist the maneuver's effects, the saving throw DC equals 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Strength or Dexterity modifier (your choice.)
  - You gain one superiority die, which is a d6 (this die is added to any superiority dice you have from another source). This die is used to fuel your maneuvers. A superiority die is expended when you use it. You regain your expended superiority dice when you finish a short or long rest.
- **Two-Weapon Fighting (PHB).** When you engage in two-weapon fighting, you can add your ability modifier to the damage of the second attack.

### Adrenaline

When you reach 2nd level, you have begun to utilize the rush of combat to your benefit. You have two Adrenaline points to fuel your abilities, which are expended upon use. You gain Adrenaline points at the end of a short or long rest, up to two. Additionally, once per turn when you succeed a weapon attack, you gain an Adrenaline point, to a maximum of three.

<div class="break"></div>
**Adrenaline save DC** = 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Wisdom modifier

### Witcher Signs

When you reach 2nd level, you have studied a selection of simple but versatile magic spells known as Signs. The Signs Known column on The Witcher table shows you how many Signs you know.  In place of an attack, you may consume an Adrenaline point to cast a Sign, which uses your Adrenaline save DC. You may cast a Sign as an action without consuming an Adrenaline point. You can only cast one Sign per round.

<div class="break"></div>
Additionally, when you gain a level in this class, you can choose one of the Witcher Signs you know and replace it with another spell from the Witcher Signs list.

#### Yrden

Place a magic trap beneath your feet with a radius of 10 ft. that slows creatures within. The circle is treated as difficult terrain for creatures of your choice. Any creatures with resistance to bludgeoning, slashing, or piercing damage must make a Wisdom saving throw when they enter the circle for the first time, losing that ability on a failed save while they remain in the radius.

<div class="break"></div>
This sign’s radius increases by 5 ft. when you reach 5th level (15 ft.), 10th level (20 ft.), and 15th level (25 ft.)

#### Quen

Conjure a barrier to protect you from incoming damage. The next time you take damage, it is reduced by 1d6 + your Wisdom modifier, and the barrier is destroyed.  

<div class="break"></div>
This sign’s power increases by 1d6 when you reach 5th level (2d6), 10th level (3d6), and 15th level (4d4)

#### Igni

Unleash a blast of flames from your hand. All creatures within a 15 ft. cone must make a Dexterity saving throw. Creatures take 1d8 fire damage on a failed save, and half as much on a success.  

<div class="break"></div>
This sign’s damage increases by 1d8 when you reach 5th level (2d8), 10th level (3d8), and 15th level (4d8)  

This sign’s range increases by 5 ft. when you reach 10th level (20 ft.).

#### Axii

Influence a creature's mind that is within 10 ft. to debilitate them. The creature must make a Charisma saving throw. On a failed save, the creature is charmed and is Dazed until the end of your next turn or when they are harmed. Creatures that are immune to being charmed automatically succeed the saving throw.  

<div class="break"></div>
This sign’s range increases by 5 ft. when you reach 5th level (15 ft.), 10th level (20 ft.), and 15th level (25 ft.)

#### Aard

Expel a telekinetic blast that sends creatures and objects away. All creatures within a 15 ft. square with the middle of an edge centered on you must make a Strength saving throw. On a failed save, creatures are pushed 10 ft. away from you and knocked prone. On a successful one, creatures are pushed 5 ft. away. Unsecured objects are also pushed 10 ft.  

<div class="break"></div>
This sign’s push distance increases by 5 ft. when you reach 5th level (15 ft.), 10th level (20 ft.), and 15th level (25 ft.). This does not affect the distance pushed on a successful save.

### Witcher School

At 3rd level, you fulfill the training of a chosen Witcher School. Your choice grants you features at 3rd level and again at 7th, 11th, and 15th, and 18th level.

### Ability Score Improvement

When you reach 4th level, and again at 8th, 12th, 16th, and 19th level, you can increase one ability score of your choice by 2, or you can increase two ability scores of your choice by 1. As normal, you can't increase an ability score above 20 using this feature.

### Extra Attack

Beginning at 5th level, you can attack twice, instead of once, whenever you take the Attack action on your turn. At 13th level, you can attack three times when you take the Attack action.

### Mutagens

Your body has fully adapted to the mutations, allowing you to further augment your abilities in the form of Mutagens.
At 6th level, you gain two Mutagens of your choice. When you gain certain Witcher levels, you gain additional Mutagens of your choice, as shown on the Witcher table. A level prerequisite refers to your level in this class.
Additionally, when you gain a level in this class, you can choose one of the Mutagen you have and replace it with another Mutagen that you could learn at that level.

### Reactive Slip

Starting at 7th level, your quickened mind allows you to mitigate the worst of danger. As a reaction to taking damage, you may consume an Adrenaline point to halve the damage taken.

### Master of the Path

At 20th level, you have become the pinnacle of a Witcher, able to take on monsters of nearly any size. Your maximum Adrenaline points increases by 2 and you now gain 3 Adrenaline points after a short or long rest.

<div class="break"></div>
When you roll a 9 or lower on a check related to recalling lore about monsters, tracking creatures, investigating areas, or performing an autopsy, you may spend an Adrenaline point to treat the roll as a 10.
