A divine warrior who channels the power of the gods through martial prowess and sacred magic.

## Class Features

### Hit Points
- **Hit Dice:** 1d10 per warpriest level
- **Hit Points at 1st Level:** 10 + your Constitution modifier
- **Hit Points at Higher Levels:** 1d10 (or 6) + your Constitution modifier per warpriest level after 1st

### Proficiencies
- **Armor:** Light armor, medium armor, shields
- **Weapons:** Simple weapons, martial weapons
- **Tools:** None
- **Saving Throws:** Wisdom, Charisma
- **Skills:** Choose two from Athletics, Insight, Intimidation, Medicine, Persuasion, and Religion

## Divine Strike

Starting at 8th level, you gain the ability to infuse your weapon strikes with divine energy. Once on each of your turns when you hit a creature with a weapon attack, you can cause the attack to deal an extra 1d8 radiant damage to the target.

## Channel Divinity

You gain the ability to channel divine energy directly from your deity, using that energy to fuel magical effects. You start with two such effects: Turn Undead and an effect determined by your domain.

### Turn Undead

As an action, you present your holy symbol and speak a prayer censuring the undead. Each undead that can see or hear you within 30 feet of you must make a Wisdom saving throw. If the creature fails its saving throw, it is turned for 1 minute or until it takes damage.

## Divine Domain

Choose one domain related to your deity: War, Life, or Tempest. Your choice grants you domain spells and other features when you choose it at 1st level.

### War Domain

The War domain represents the divine power of war and conquest. Warpriests of this domain are fierce warriors who use their divine magic to enhance their martial prowess.

**Domain Spells:** You gain domain spells at the warpriest levels noted in the War Domain Spells table.

| Warpriest Level | Spells |
|----------------|--------|
| 1st | divine favor, shield of faith |
| 3rd | magic weapon, spiritual weapon |
| 5th | crusader's mantle, spirit guardians |
| 7th | freedom of movement, guardian of faith |
| 9th | flame strike, hold monster |
