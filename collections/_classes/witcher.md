***Normal individuals turned freaks of nature by the horrendous experiments known as the Trial of Grasses, Witchers are professional monster hunters who traditionally seek out contracts for coin. They are trained at a young age to be expert swordsmen, to memorize alchemical recipes and bestiarys, and to cast minor spells known as Signs to overcome their foes. Some beings despise their existence, many fear the twin blades on their back, but all cry for a Witcher when a monster threatens their lives.***

*You must have a Dexterity score or Strength score and a Wisdom score of 13 or higher in order to multiclass in or out of this class.*

## Class Features

As a Witcher, you gain the following class features.

### Hit Points

**Hit Dice:** 1d10 per Witcher level

**Hit Points at 1st Level:** 10 + your Constitution modifier

**Hit Points at Higher Levels:** 1d10 (or 6) + your Constitution modifier per Witcher level after 1st

### Proficiencies

**Armor:** Light armor, medium armor, heavy armor, shields

**Weapons:** Simple weapons, martial weapons

**Tools:** Alchemist’s Supplies

**Saving Throws:** Dexterity, Wisdom

**Skills:** Choose three from Acrobatics, Arcana, Athletics, History, Insight, Intimidation, Investigation, Medicine, Nature, Perception, Survival

### Equipment

You start with the following equipment, in addition to the equipment granted by your background:

- (a) scale mail or (b) leather armor
- (a) a steel longsword
  (a) a silver longsword or (b) a silvered martial weapon
- (a) a dungeoneer's pack or (b) an explorer's pack
- A dagger
- Alchemist’s supplies

### Mutations

Beginning at 1st level, you have undergone the horrific Trial of the Grasses, which has irrevocably altered your body. You gain the following benefits:

- Your pupils have changed to look like that of a cat, granting you darkvision, up to a range of 30 ft.
- You have advantage on Wisdom (Perception) and Wisdom (survival) checks based on sight or smell.
- You may add your proficiency bonus to Initiative rolls.
- You are immune to most diseases.
- The trials have rendered you sterile, preventing you from having children.
- You age dramatically slower, extending your lifespan by up to 5 times.

At 9th level, your mutations develop further, granting you the following additional benefits:

- Your darkvision is increased to a range of 60 ft.
- Your walking speed increases by 10 ft.
- Your blindsight range is increased by 10 ft.

### Alchemy

You have spent many hours studying various alchemical mixes to create potions and oils to aid you on the path. At 1st level, you know three [recipes](#alchemy-brews-list) from the list found at the end of the class description. At the end of a short or long rest, you may brew a number of concoctions equal to your proficiency bonus with Alchemist’s supplies. Some brews could be from the same recipe, though you can only carry 2 uses of a certain recipe at once, and only Witchers can drink the potions you create.

<div class="break"></div>
Despite your mutations allowing you to consume your potions, they are still dangerous in large quantities. 
- **If you have two Witcher potions active at the same time:** You begin taking 1d6 + your proficiency bonus poison damage at the start of your turn until both potion effects wear off. 
- **If you have three Witcher potions active at the same time:** You are Poisoned, take 1d10 + your proficiency bonus poison damage at the start of your turn, and healing is halved until all potion effects wear off. At this stage, you can not drink another potion.

When you level up, you may choose an additional brew to learn from the list or you may enhance an existing brew you know.

### Fighting Style

You adopt a particular style of fighting as your specialty. Choose one of the following options. You can't take a Fighting Style option more than once, even if you later get to choose again.

- **Blind Fighting (TCE).** You have blindsight with a range of 10 feet. Within that range, you can effectively see anything that isn't behind total cover, even if you're blinded or in darkness. Moreover, you can see an invisible creature within that range, unless the creature successfully hides from you.
- **Defense (PHB).** While you are wearing armor, you gain a +1 bonus to AC.
- **Dueling (PHB).** When you are wielding a melee weapon in one hand and no other weapons, you gain a +2 bonus to damage rolls with that weapon.
- **Great Weapon Fighting (PHB).** When you roll a 1 or 2 on a damage die for an attack you make with a melee weapon that you are wielding with two hands, you can reroll the die and must use the new roll, even if the new roll is a 1 or a 2. The weapon must have the two-handed or versatile property for you to gain this benefit.
- **Interception (TCE).** When a creature you can see hits a target, other than you, within 5 feet of you with an attack, you can use your reaction to reduce the damage the target takes by 1d10 + your proficiency bonus (to a minimum of 0 damage). You must be wielding a shield or a simple or martial weapon to use this reaction.
- **Protection (PHB).** When a creature you can see attacks a target other than you that is within 5 feet of you, you can use your reaction to impose disadvantage on the attack roll. You must be wielding a shield.
- **Superior Technique (TCE).** You learn one maneuver of your choice from among those available to the Battle Master archetype. If a maneuver you use requires your target to make a saving throw to resist the maneuver's effects, the saving throw DC equals 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Strength or Dexterity modifier (your choice.)
  - You gain one superiority die, which is a d6 (this die is added to any superiority dice you have from another source). This die is used to fuel your maneuvers. A superiority die is expended when you use it. You regain your expended superiority dice when you finish a short or long rest.
- **Two-Weapon Fighting (PHB).** When you engage in two-weapon fighting, you can add your ability modifier to the damage of the second attack.

### Adrenaline

When you reach 2nd level, you have begun to utilize the rush of combat to your benefit. You have two Adrenaline points to fuel your abilities, which are expended upon use. You gain Adrenaline points at the end of a short or long rest, up to two. Additionally, once per turn when you succeed a weapon attack, you gain an Adrenaline point, to a maximum of three.

<div class="break"></div>
**Adrenaline save DC** = 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Wisdom modifier

### Witcher Signs

When you reach 2nd level, you have studied a selection of simple but versatile magic spells known as Signs. The Signs Known column on The Witcher table shows you how many Signs you know.  In place of an attack, you may consume an Adrenaline point to cast a Sign, which uses your Adrenaline save DC. You may cast a Sign as an action without consuming an Adrenaline point. You can only cast one Sign per round.

<div class="break"></div>
Additionally, when you gain a level in this class, you can choose one of the Witcher Signs you know and replace it with another spell from the Witcher Signs list.

#### [Yrden](/dnd/spells/yrden)

Place a magic trap beneath your feet with a radius of 10 ft. that slows creatures within. The circle is treated as difficult terrain for creatures of your choice. Any creatures with resistance to bludgeoning, slashing, or piercing damage must make a Wisdom saving throw when they enter the circle for the first time, losing that ability on a failed save while they remain in the radius.

<div class="break"></div>
This sign’s radius increases by 5 ft. when you reach 5th level (15 ft.), 10th level (20 ft.), and 15th level (25 ft.)

#### [Quen](/dnd/spells/quen)

Conjure a barrier to protect you from incoming damage. The next time you take damage, it is reduced by 1d6 + your Wisdom modifier, and the barrier is destroyed.  

<div class="break"></div>
This sign’s power increases by 1d6 when you reach 5th level (2d6), 10th level (3d6), and 15th level (4d4)

#### [Igni](/dnd/spells/igni)

Unleash a blast of flames from your hand. All creatures within a 15 ft. cone must make a Dexterity saving throw. Creatures take 1d8 fire damage on a failed save, and half as much on a success.  

<div class="break"></div>
This sign’s damage increases by 1d8 when you reach 5th level (2d8), 10th level (3d8), and 15th level (4d8)  

This sign’s range increases by 5 ft. when you reach 10th level (20 ft.).

#### [Axii](/dnd/spells/axii)

Influence a creature's mind that is within 10 ft. to debilitate them. The creature must make a Charisma saving throw. On a failed save, the creature is charmed and is Dazed until the end of your next turn or when they are harmed. Creatures that are immune to being charmed automatically succeed the saving throw.  

<div class="break"></div>
This sign’s range increases by 5 ft. when you reach 5th level (15 ft.), 10th level (20 ft.), and 15th level (25 ft.)

#### [Aard](/dnd/spells/aard)

Expel a telekinetic blast that sends creatures and objects away. All creatures within a 15 ft. square with the middle of an edge centered on you must make a Strength saving throw. On a failed save, creatures are pushed 10 ft. away from you and knocked prone. On a successful one, creatures are pushed 5 ft. away. Unsecured objects are also pushed 10 ft.  

<div class="break"></div>
This sign’s push distance increases by 5 ft. when you reach 5th level (15 ft.), 10th level (20 ft.), and 15th level (25 ft.). This does not affect the distance pushed on a successful save.

### Witcher School

At 3rd level, you fulfill the training of a chosen Witcher School. Your choice grants you features at 3rd level and again at 7th, 11th, and 15th, and 18th level.

### Ability Score Improvement

When you reach 4th level, and again at 8th, 12th, 16th, and 19th level, you can increase one ability score of your choice by 2, or you can increase two ability scores of your choice by 1. As normal, you can't increase an ability score above 20 using this feature.

### Extra Attack

Beginning at 5th level, you can attack twice, instead of once, whenever you take the Attack action on your turn. At 13th level, you can attack three times when you take the Attack action.

### Mutagens

Your body has fully adapted to the mutations, allowing you to further augment your abilities in the form of [Mutagens](#mutagens-list).

<div class="break"></div>

At 6th level, you gain two [Mutagens](#mutagens-list) of your choice. When you gain certain Witcher levels, you gain additional Mutagens of your choice, as shown on the Witcher table. A level prerequisite refers to your level in this class.
Additionally, when you gain a level in this class, you can choose one of the Mutagen you have and replace it with another Mutagen that you could learn at that level.

### Reactive Slip

Starting at 7th level, your quickened mind allows you to mitigate the worst of danger. As a reaction to taking damage, you may consume an Adrenaline point to halve the damage taken.

### Master of the Path

At 20th level, you have become the pinnacle of a Witcher, able to take on monsters of nearly any size. Your maximum Adrenaline points increases by 2 and you now gain 3 Adrenaline points after a short or long rest.

<div class="break"></div>
When you roll a 9 or lower on a check related to recalling lore about monsters, tracking creatures, investigating areas, or performing an autopsy, you may spend an Adrenaline point to treat the roll as a 10.

## Alchemy Brews

### Black Blood
A dark, viscous liquid that tastes of iron and mold. When you drink this potion, for 1 minute creatures that attack you with a melee attack take 1d8 + your proficiency bonus necrotic damage.

<div class="break"></div>

The enhanced version increases the damage by 1d8 (2d8) 

### Blade Oil
A thick, smelly oil made of materials that actively harm a specific creature type. When you make this brew, you must choose a creature type. When applied to a weapon as a bonus action, your attacks against that creature type with the weapon deal an additional 1d6 damage until the end of your next long rest. For humanoid creature types, you must specify the race.

<div class="break"></div>

The enhanced version increases the damage to 1d8.

### Blizzard
A deep aqua mixture that greatly quickens the mind. For 1 minute after drinking this potion, once per round you may gain an additional action and half your movement speed is restored when you bring a creature below 0 hit points.

<div class="break"></div>

The enhanced version also grants an additional bonus action.

### Cat
A slightly bubbly grey liquid that widens your pupils past their usual limit. For 1 minute after drinking this potion, your darkvision range is tripled and you no longer have disadvantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks against creatures that are lightly obscured.

<div class="break"></div>

The enhanced version increases the critical hit range on weapon attacks by 1.

### Full Moon
A dark blue liquid that smells slightly of flowers. For 1 minute after drinking this potion, you hit points and maximum hit points are increased by 2d8.

<div class="break"></div>

The enhanced version increases your hit points and maximum hit points by 3d10.

### Golden Oriole
A shining golden brew made from sun-bathed seeds and herbs. When you drink this potion, you are no longer poisoned and have resistance against poison damage and the Poisoned condition for 
1 minute.

<div class="break"></div>

The enhanced version grants immunity to poison damage and the Poisoned condition. This does not affect the maximum number of brews you may imbibe at once, but does negate the negative effects of having so many active at once.


### Killer Whale
A bright orange liquid that tastes strongly of grease and oil. For 1 hour when you drink this potion, the duration you can hold your breath for and survive without air is doubled.

<div class="break"></div>

The enhanced version fully removes the need for air and you gain a swimming speed equal to your walking speed.

### Maribor Forest
A slimy, transparent mixture that heightens your body's generation of chemicals. For 1 minute when you drink this potion, you may gain an Adrenaline point on weapon attacks even if you miss.

<div class="break"></div>

The enhanced version grants you an Adrenaline point when you drink it.


### Petri's Philter
An opaque blue drink that increases the magical power of your Signs. For 1 minute when you drink this potion, you may spend an Adrenaline point when casting a Sign to improve its effectiveness by the amount shown in the appropriate Sign description.

<div class="break"></div>

The enhanced version no longer requires you to spend an Adrenaline point.

### Swallow
A thin, red liquid that tastes heavily of congealed blood. For 1 minute when you drink this potion, you gain 1d4 + your proficiency bonus hit points at the start of your turn. If you are reduced to 0 hit points, you will no longer heal until you regain hit points, though you have advantage on death saving throws.

<div class="break"></div>

The enhanced version grants you 2d4 + your proficiency bonus hit points at the start of your turn and you automatically succeed death saving throws.

### Tawny Owl
A mixture that is a vibrant purple. For 1 minute when you drink this potion, you gain an Adrenaline point at the start of your turn.

<div class="break"></div>

The enhanced version increases your maximum Adrenaline points by 2.

### Thunderbolt
An emerald green mixture made from bug larvae. For 1 minute when you drink this potion, once per round you may reroll the damage die of a weapon attack, taking the higher roll.

<div class="break"></div>

The enhanced version allows you to add your proficiency bonus to the damage when you reroll a weapon attack.

### White Honey
A sweet drink that neutralizes the effects of other brews. When you drink this potion, all potion effects, poisons, and toxins are rendered ineffective.

<div class="break"></div>

The enhanced version allows this potion to be imbibed by creatures that are not Witchers.

### White Raffard's Decoction
A hearty, white liquid that instantly repairs parts of your body. When you drink this potion, you regain 10 + your proficiency bonus hit points. The toxins of this potion remain for 1 minute after drinking.

<div class="break"></div>

The enhanced version grants you 25 + your proficiency bonus hit points.

### Wolven Hour
A thick mixture made from canine teeth and monster guts. For 1 minute when you drink this potion, you may treat a magic item you are carrying that you are not attuned to as attuned. You must have an empty attunement slot to do this.

<div class="break"></div>

The enhanced version allows you to use the effect even without an empty attunement slot.

## Mutagens

### Assembly Alchemy
You become more efficient at creating brews, allowing you to brew an additional three concoctions at the end of a short or long rest using Alchemist’s supplies.

### Conditioned Stomach
*Prerequisite: 9th level*

<div class="break"></div>

Your body is now better adapted to the toxicity of your brews. The negative effects of Witcher brews are delayed to three and four brews at once. However, you now take 1d10 + your proficiency bonus poison damage in the first stage and 1d12 + your proficiency poison damage in the second stage.

### Refreshment
When you drink a potion, you regain 2d6 + your proficiency bonus hit points.

### Poisoned Blade
The oil applied to your weapons are now also laced with toxic chemicals. When you succeed in a weapon attack with an oiled weapon, the target must make a Constitution saving throw against your Adrenaline save DC. Upon fail, they are Poisoned for 1 minute. They may reroll the Constitution saving throw at the end of their turn.

### Killing Spree
*Prerequisite: 9th level*

<div class="break"></div>

You’ve learned to allow the toxins in your brews to blur out the unimportant distractions in your mind while maintaining focus on the fight. While you have at least two Witcher potions active at once, your critical hit range on weapon attacks is increased by 1 and you gain advantage on your first weapon attack per turn.

### Whirl
*Prerequisite: 9th level*

As an action, you consume an Adrenaline point to spin the blade in a well-practiced manner that almost looks like a dance. You may make a weapon attack against all creatures within your weapons reach. To continue spinning your blade, you must spend an Adrenaline point and your action at the start of your turn. While spinning, any creature that starts their turn or enters your melee range must make a Dexterity saving throw against your Adrenaline save DC. Upon a failure, they are struck by the weapon. When you take damage, you make Concentration checks to maintain your form. Falling Prone, becoming Restrained, or Incapacitated automatically ends your concentration.

### Rend
*Prerequisite: 9th level*

<div class="break"></div>

As part of a weapon attack, consume a number of Adrenaline points to focus your energy and strength into the strike. If the attack hits, you deal an additional d8 of damage for each Adrenaline point consumed.

### Counterattack
When a creature misses an attack roll against you, you may spend an Adrenaline point to gain advantage on your next weapon attack against that creature.

### Crippling Strikes
When you succeed an attack against a creature, you may spend an Adrenaline point to inflict them with Bleeding for 1 minute.

### Sunder Armor
As part of a weapon attack, you may expend an Adrenaline point to attempt to break through a creature’s armor. On a hit, all melee attacks against that creature have advantage until the end of your next turn.

### Razor Focus
Your body’s natural reactions to danger kick in immediately. You gain an Adrenaline point when you roll initiative.

### Aard Sweep
You can now push the ground beneath you when you cast Aard, centering the square on yourself and targeting all creatures around you.

### Force Field
The leftover energy of your Aard now forms a thin barrier around you, hindering creatures moving toward you. In a range of 15 ft., creatures attempting to move towards you treat the ground as difficult terrain.

### Pyromaniac
The flames of Igni now catch creatures on fire. Creatures that fail the saving throw also take fire damage equal to your proficiency bonus at the start of their turn until they use their action to put themselves out or are submerged in water.

### Fire Stream
You learn an alternate way of casting Igni, instead firing a constant stream of fire from your hand. This alternate version’s area of effect is now a line of the same range, though after casting it once, you may cast it again as a weapon attack without consuming an Adrenaline point for the next minute, even in the same turn. The spell ends if you do anything as an action that is not this alternative casting of Igni.

### Magic Trap
Yrden now saps that energy of creatures caught within it. All creatures of your choice that start their turn or enter the circle take 1d4 + your proficiency force damage.

### No Escape
You learn an alternate way of casting Yrden, which instead of slowing and removing the protections of creatures, instead places a barrier around the border of the spell to prevent escape. When a creature inside Yrden attempts to leave its range, it must make a Strength saving throw against your Adrenaline Save DC. On a fail, they are unable to leave its radius until their next turn, where they may reroll the saving throw.

### Exploding Shield
Quen’s barrier now unleashes a concussive blast when broken. When you take damage with Quen active, all creatures within 5 ft. of you must make a Strength saving throw. On a fail, they are knocked prone and pushed 10 ft. away. On a success, they are pushed 5 ft. away.

### Expanded Shield
You learn an alternate way of casting Quen, where the shield can be placed onto others or protect all creatures within an enclosed area, at the cost of immediate protection. This alternate version now has a range of Touch and protects all creatures and objects within the same space. You can spend an additional Adrenaline point to expand the shield to a 10 ft. sphere.

### Delusion
Axii can now give you an edge in social situations. When you cast Axii on a creature, you can instead choose to dull its wits and treat you as a friendly acquaintance. You get advantage on Charisma (Persuasion) and Charisma (Deception) checks until the Sign ends. Creatures that are hostile against you have advantage on the saving throw when you use this alternate version. The creature is aware of your charm once the sign ends.

### Turn Mind
You learn an alternate way of casting Axii, which instead of debilitating a creature’s mind, it bends it to your side. This alternate version causes the targeted creature to regard you and your companions as allies, while seeing its own allies as enemies, forcing it to attack them or otherwise be hostile towards them. While Turned, the creature may remake the saving throw when it takes damage. You must spend an Adrenaline point at the start of subsequent turns or else the spell ends. This version of Axii requires Concentration or else the spell ends.
