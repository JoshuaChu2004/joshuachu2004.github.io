The Valkyries are said to scour battlefields and places of conflict in search of souls who fell in honorable combat.

<div class="break"></div>

This spear was found stuck in the center of an ancient tree stump. It had a string of Sylvan words around the tip that read out "Warriors, heed the Valkyrie's call. Wield the spear of skies as your companions lay their arms to rest, and face your final test". Upon defeating the Spirit Valkyrie that appeared, it left behind the spear as a reward.

<div class="break"></div>

The spear is full sized, constructed out of a Sisterine Oak handle and a Asgrian Steel tip. The spear blade is constructed in a V-shape with a triangular symbol engraved on both sides. It is quite light and seems to have a certain control over lightning.

<div class="break"></div>

**Wide Sweep.** Using up a charge, you can make change your attack action to perform a sweep that strikes all creatures in a semi-circle with radius of 10 ft. in any direction of your choosing. All creatures caught within the radius much make a DEX save with DC equal to your To Hit Roll. On fail, they are hit with the spear. On success, they take no damage.

<div class="break"></div>

**Skyward Crash.** Using up a charge, you may electrify your spear as an action. All subsequent hits with the spear for the next minute have an added 1d6 Lightning damage.

<div class="break"></div>

**Recall.** Upon throwing the spear, you may call it back into your hand as a bonus action.

<div class="break"></div>

The spear is also a +1 magical weapon.
