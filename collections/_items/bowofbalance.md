A beautifully crafted bow made of elven wood and a blessed metal core. It is well used amongst followers as a weapon of war and a tool to hunt. It boasts extreme accuracy and consistency while also having magical properties for more devout followers.

<div class="break"></div>

**Split Arrow.** Upon hitting an attack with the bow, consume a charge to cause the arrows to dissolve into two glowing bolts that act similarly to magic missiles, dealing an additional 1d4+1 force damage for each missile on any creature within 20 ft. of the target.

<div class="break"></div>

**Guided Arrow.** When attacking with the bow, consume a charge to bless it with divine energy so that it automatically seeks the opponent, giving you advantage on the hit.

<div class="break"></div>

This weapon is a +2 magical weapon.
