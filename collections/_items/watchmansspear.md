A spear constructed out of a bronze allow with a steel tip, this weapon is commonly found in the hands of city guards or soldiers who protect their fellow comrades or citizens.

<div class="break"></div>

Upon a successful hit, you may choose to Restrain the targeted creature by leaving your spear inside of them. The creature must make a DC 16 STR Save. The creature may reroll this Save as an action to remove the spear. Upon a success, the spear is returned to the user's hand (or feet if no hands are free).

<div class="break"></div>

This weapon is a +1 magical weapon.
