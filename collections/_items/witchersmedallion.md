This amulet bears the symbol of an animal, representing the Witcher school it was forged for. While you wear the witcher's symbol, you gain a bonus to Adrenaline save DC of your Witcher abilities. The bonus is determined by the medallion's rarity.

<div class="break"></div>

While you wear this medallion, it hums to arcane auras. You can sense the presence of non-Humanoid creatures within 30 ft. You can also detect the presence of magic (as per the detect magic spell) within 30 feet. This does not reveal the school of magic or the source unless you can see it.
