A hidden shield magically compacted and concealed within a form of wristwear (bracelet, watch, etc.). When the mechanism is activated, the shield expands to protect the user from attack. The shield has a center of bronze with the outside rim made of a transparent magical force to decrease blindspots. The mechanism can be activated as a bonus action or as a reaction, adding 2 to the user's AC.
