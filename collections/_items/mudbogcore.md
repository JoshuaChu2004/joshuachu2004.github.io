A core of pure elemental power, taken from the heart of a Mudbog. This small, rock-like trinket is able to grant the user control over the very ground they stand on.

<div class="break"></div>

The attuned user has access to the cantrip Mold Earth, and the spells Earth Tremor, Earth Bind, and Erupting Earth. These spells have a Save DC of 15. The core has 5 charges and casting a levelled spell uses a number of charges equal to the spells level. The core regains a d4 of charges at every dawn.
