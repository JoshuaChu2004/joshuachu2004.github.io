A platinum circlet with a wreath of leaves, created by Pendragon, God of Kings, as a holy relic. Its power grants you the ability to gain the respect of those around you and be treated as a leader. The amount of charges directly scales with the amount of people under your will.

<div class="break"></div>

**Charismatic Leader.** Your Charisma score is increased by 1.

<div class="break"></div>

**Command DC.** Your Command DC is equal to your Charisma Score - 2.

<div class="break"></div>

**Followers.** Humanoid/Sentient creatures that either trust you/see you as an ally or are under the effects of Command Respect. The number of charges in the circlet is equal to the number of followers you have, which unlocks certain abilities under the pillars of rulership. Followers not under the effects of Command Respect are not forced to follow your orders but are still susceptible to Command Respect.

<div class="break"></div>

**Command Respect.** You are able to attempt to magically influence a creature to have an immense amount of respect for you. A creature can intentionally fall under this effect, but if it chooses to resist, they must pass a Wisdom save with DC equal to your Charisma Score. While under your command, they will follow orders that are reasonable and somewhat in line with their own morals (they are unlikely to commit murder or steal when they otherwise would not do so). Creatures who are hostile, harbor strong dislike towards you, or are keenly aware of the circlet, have advantage on the Wisdom save. A creature can attempt to break free from your will once a day, requiring the same Charisma score. You may only attempt this feature on the same creature once a day.

<div class="break"></div>

**NOTE:** This effect is much greater than a simple charm, so Elves and similar creatures are still affected.

<div class="break"></div>

**Pillar of Rulership.** There are three pillars of rulership, all of which are critical to becoming an excellent monarch. Them being Strength, Bravery, and Intellect. At the end of each long rest, you may choose one pillar to focus on. Each pillar grants you additional abilities that affect those under your command. Additional abilities are usable once you acquire enough subjects.

<div class="break"></div>

**Strength.** Every ruler must show strength so that their power remains unchallenged.

<div class="break"></div>

**Empowered Strike (1 Follower, 1 Charge).** As a bonus action, you strengthen a follower's or your next attack, giving it advantage on the hit roll.

<div class="break"></div>

**Death Sentence (5 Followers, 4 Charges).** As an action, you mark a certain target for death. For the next round, any attacks against that target have advantage on the damage roll and Hit Rolls have Improved Critical (19-20). Creatures that already have Improved Critical have their crit. range extended by 1.

<div class="break"></div>

**Bravery.** Every ruler must show bravery so that those around them may fight with as much courage.

<div class="break"></div>

**Unyielding Courage (1 Follower, N Charges).** As a bonus action, you give a number of creatures equal to the number of charges you choose immense courage, which gives them advantage on any saves made against the Frightened condition and all Intimidation checks for the next minute.

<div class="break"></div>

**Rally (4 Followers, 3 Charges).** As an action, you give a war shout and instill courage into your followers. Any followers that are within 60 ft. and can hear you gain Temp HP equal to your Charisma modifier and any creature below half-HP gain Inspiration every turn for 1 minute. Rally only works in life-or-death combat, meaning it does not work outside of initiative.

<div class="break"></div>

**Intellect.** Every ruler must show intellect so that their decisions are sound and are trusted by their subjects.

<div class="break"></div>

**Judgement (2 Followers, 2 Charge).** You look into the heart of a creature of your choice and can determine their general feelings/thoughts at the moment.

<div class="break"></div>

**Settle Conflict (6 Followers, 4 Charges).** You influence other creatures to view you as extremely wise. Non-followers must make a Wisdom Save equal to your Will DC. Upon fail, they agree to allow you be the judge in a conflict or dispute that is happening. Whichever side you choose or however you make a compromise (within reason), both parties will be satisfied and agree to the terms.
