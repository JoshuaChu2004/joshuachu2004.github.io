A simple platinum ring, with runes of topaz dust carved on the outside, it carries the power of the celestial light. While attuned, any creature struck by a Divine Smite must make a CON Save against the user's paladin Spell Save DC. Upon fail, they are blinded until the end of the user's next turn.

<div class="break"></div>

This feature may be used three times before needing to recharge at the next dawn.
