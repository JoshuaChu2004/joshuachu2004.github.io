A very beautifully made shield blessed with divine energy. When the user plants to shield into the ground and speaks its command word, the shield produces a transparent wall that is 20 ft. wide and 15 ft. tall. The wall has an AC of 15, has 30 hit points, and provides full cover to creatures behind it. After 1 minute or if the wall loses all of its hit points, the wall shatters and the shield returns to the wrist of the user. This feature can only be used once per long rest.
