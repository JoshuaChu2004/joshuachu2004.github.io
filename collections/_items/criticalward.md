An amulet worn by adventures around the world that is able to protect the user from an enemy's most powerful attacks. When an attack against you is a critical hit, the attacker is forced to reroll its attack roll. If the new roll is also a critical hit, the amulet shatters. An attack can only be affected by a Critical Ward once.
