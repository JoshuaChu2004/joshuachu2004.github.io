A very crude and unreliable invention. It simply consists a steel tube with a hole to insert a fuse. Ammunition and gunpowder is loaded through the bore.

<div class="break"></div>

Upon misfiring, the gun completely explodes, dealing 2d6 Fire damage to the user and sending all projectiles in random directions. If using ball bearings (or many projectiles), all creatures within 15 ft. take 1d6 piercing damage (including the user). If using only a few projectiles, they fire off in random directions at the DM's choice. Any targetted creatures must make a DEX save with DC 18 to avoid the shot.
