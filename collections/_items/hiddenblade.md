A strange contraption that fits around your wrist. When you flick your hand back, a small blade shoots forward, extending past your hand. Another flick and the blade disappears once more. When the attuned user wears it, the item will magically change shape and color to blend in with your sleeve (or skin). A creature investigating the wrist must roll Investigation DC 16 to notice the blade, having disadvantage if the attuned user is wearing it.

<div class="break"></div>

While this weapon acts as a normal dagger, it can also be used to easily assassinate or severely damage targets when in hiding.

<div class="break"></div>

**Sneak Strike.** When an attack is made while you are hidden, you may add 4d6 piercing damage to the attack. If you are hidden but the target is aware of your presence Sneak Strike deals half damage.

<div class="break"></div>

**Assassinate.** When you are hidden, you may attempt to assassinate a target. By plunging the blade in a critical area, most targets are likely to instantly die by the attack. First, the user must roll a Stealth check with DC equal to the enemy's passive perception. Upon success, the user can then make an attack with the weapon (without advantage from stealth). If the Hit succeeds and is greater than or equal to 16 + (2*CR), the creature will automatically die. If the Hit is lower but still succeeds, the attack is a critical hit, and Sneak Strike is added. If the user fails the initial Stealth check, the attack is still made but without advantage or Sneak Strike.
