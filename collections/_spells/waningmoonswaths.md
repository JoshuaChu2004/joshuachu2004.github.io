You sheath your weapon and prepare to strike, your stance reflecting the waning moon. Until the start of your next turn, you gain a +3 bonus to AC, which lasts until you make your next melee weapon attack. For each attack that misses you while this stance is active, your next melee weapon attack before the end of your next turn deals an extra 1d4 radiant damage.

<div class="break"></div>

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, the radiant damage die increases to a d6 at 2nd level, a d8 at 3rd level, a d10 at 4th level, and a d12 at 5th level.
