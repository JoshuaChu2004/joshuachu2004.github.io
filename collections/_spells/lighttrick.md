You create two afterimages of yourself that look, move, and feel like you to confuse and cause mayhem. The images take up their own space and can move equal to your speed on your turn. The images also count as an allied creature for effects such as Flanking. When an image takes damage, it promptly explodes, forcing any creatures within 15 ft. of it to make a Dexterity saving throw. Upon fail, they take 2d6 cosmic damage or half as much on a success.

**Star Charged.** You summon an additional clone. As a bonus action, you can command a clone to explode.
