Conjure up an image of the night sky filled with brilliant light from galaxies and systems beyond. Each creatures that can see the image of your choosing must make a Wisdom saving throw. Upon fail, they are Dazed until the spell ends. They may also reroll their Saving Throw at the end of their turn. A creature that succeeds its save becomes immune to the effects of this spell for 24 hours.

**Star Charged.** Creatures can no longer reroll the Saving Throw at the end of their turn.
