Your body briefly transforms into the body of a powerful fiend. Your skin turns a deep crimson, wings sprout from your back, and a set of horns grows on your head. You gain a fly speed of 60 ft. and your magical flames become hellfire, causing it to ignore any resistances to fire damage the creatures affected by it may have.
<div class="break"></div>

**At Higher Levels.** When casted using a spell slot of 7th level or higher, your flames also ignore immunity to fire damage.
