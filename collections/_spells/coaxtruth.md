You magically manipulate a targeted creature's mind to answer the question you ask truthfully. The creature must make a Wisdom saving throw. Upon fail, they are Charmed and compelled to answer truthfully. Upon success, the creature may answer (or not answer) however they like. Creatures with immunity to being Charmed or with an Intelligence score of less than 4 are not affected by this spell. At the DM's discretion, the creature may automatically succeed the save if the answer is a greatly guarded secret. The target has advantage on saving throws against this spell until the next Dawn.
