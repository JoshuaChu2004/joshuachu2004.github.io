You begin to influence gravity's direction on a creature or object of at most large size, forcing it to make a Strength saving throw. On a fail, you can change the direction of gravity for the target. You may even choose to remove gravity, causing them to remain suspended in the air. At the end of its turn, the creature may make a Strength saving throw to end the spell. This spell ends if the object or creature is ever further than 120 ft. from you.

As a bonus action on subsequent turns, you can change the direction of gravity again.

**Star Charged.** Affected creatures take an additional 1d6 bludgeoning damage from falling.
