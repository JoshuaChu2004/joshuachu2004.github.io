Begin to store tremendous amounts of power into your weapon to unleash into one cataclysmic strike. In place of a weapon attack, you may choose to store additional energy, increasing its total damage. Your next weapon attack that hits unleashes all of the stored energy, dealing 6d10 fire damage and an additional d10 of fire damage for each weapon attack used to store energy. This spell automatically ends when you fall below 0 hit points or are no longer holding the weapon.
<div class="break"></div>

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 6th level or higher, the base damage increases by 1d10 for each slot level above 5th.
