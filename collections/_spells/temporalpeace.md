Completely stop time for a short moment. During this time, you can not move farther than 15 ft. from your space or interact creatures. Before this spell ends, you go back to where you had casted the spell and any items you have interacted with return to their original state and location. You can look around and make mental or sense checks such as Perception, Investigation, and Arcana. After casting this cantrip, you cannot cast it again for 1 minute.
<div class="break"></div>

This cantrip's sphere radius increases by 5 ft. when you reach 5th level (20 ft.), 10th level (25 ft.), and 15th level (30 ft.).
