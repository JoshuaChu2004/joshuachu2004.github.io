Strike out at those around you in a circle. Choose up to two creatures within 15 ft. of you. Make a melee weapon attack against each creature. On a hit, they are pushed 5 ft. away.

<div class="break"></div>

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, you may target an additional creature for each slot level above 1st.
