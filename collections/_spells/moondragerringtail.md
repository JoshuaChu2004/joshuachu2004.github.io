Perform a low, circular slash like a dragon's tail swipe. All creatures within 10 ft. of you must make a Strength saving throw. On a fail, they take 1d8 damage of your weapon's type and fall Prone. On a success, they take half damage.

<div class="break"></div>

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, the damage increases by 1d8 for each slot level above 1st.
