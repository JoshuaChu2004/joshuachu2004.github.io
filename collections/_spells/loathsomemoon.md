Conjure the weight of the blood moon and brandish your weapon. Choose a creature within 60 ft. that you can see and force it to make a Charisma saving throw. On a fail, the next time you hit the target with a melee weapon attack before this spell ends, they must spend their next turn attempting to harm you in some way.

<div class="break"></div>

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, you may target an additional creature for each slot level above 1st.
