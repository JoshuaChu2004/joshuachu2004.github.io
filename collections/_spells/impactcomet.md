You sheath your weapon and prepare a devastating strike. The next attack that hits a creature deals an additional 1d8 cosmic damage and they must make a Strength saving throw. On a fail, they are pushed 5 ft. back and knocked Prone.

<div class="break"></div>

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, the damage increases by 1d8 for each slot level above 1st.
