title: Immovable Weapon
school: Transmutation
level: 5th
casting_time: 1 action
range: Self
duration: Until Dispelled or Triggered
components: V, S, M (a magical weapon)
ritual: false
concentration: false

 - list: Armswright

You enchant a magical weapon and drive it into a solid surface, where it remains fixed by arcane force. The weapon cannot be moved, regardless of weight or leverage, unless the magic is broken.

<div class="break"></div>

When you cast this spell, you may designate creatures that can pull the weapon out or a condition a creature must meet in order to pull it out (such as a password, characteristic, or magical power).
    
<div class="break"></div>

Any other creature attempting to remove the weapon must succeed a DC 25 Strength check to dislodge the weapon, ending the spell.
