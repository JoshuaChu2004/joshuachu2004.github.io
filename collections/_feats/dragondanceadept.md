You’ve become proficient in the Dragon Dance form of fighting, which involves constantly shifting your body and position to be able to dodge any incoming attacks. From the outside, it looks like a festive dance.
- Increase your Dexterity score by 1, up to a maximum of 20.
- **Draconic Riposte.** Whenever a creature makes a melee attack against you and misses, you manage to perfectly dodge their attack, allowing you to make a melee attack as a reaction.
