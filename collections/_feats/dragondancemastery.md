You are now a master of the Dragon Dance, allowing you to assume two new stances for greater versatility in combat.
- Increase Dexterity score by 1, max of 20
- **Stance Dance.** Having become a master of Dragon Dance, you are now able to switch between three stances: the offensive Champion Stance, the neutral Dragon Stance, and the defensive Vanguard Stance. You can switch between these three stances when you succeed a melee attack or when you take damage as a reaction. All bonuses and detriments from stances are lost upon exiting that stance.
- **Champion Stance.** An offensive stance that sacrifices defence in favor of more power. Upon entering this stance, you have advantage on your next attack and may reroll a damage die on your weapon attack rolls. However, other creatures have advantage on their attacks against you. You can switch to Dragon Stance without succeeding a melee attack as a minor action.  Instead of Draconic Riposte, enemies who miss a melee attack now trigger Champion Deflect.
    - **Champion Deflect.** After performing a risky deflection of your opponent's attack, you place yourself in an advantageous position to strike at a critical spot. Your next melee attack against the creature deals an additional damage die. You may switch your stance.
- **Dragon Stance.** The neutral stance that provides adequate offensive capabilities and defence. Once per round upon entering this stance, you gain 10 ft. of movement.
    - **Draconic Riposte.** You may switch your stance even if you miss your melee attack.
- **Vanguard Stance.** A defensive stance that sacrifices power for increased protection. Upon entering this stance, you gain a bonus to your AC and saving throws equal to your Dexterity modifier. Each time you are attacked or make a saving throw, this bonus decreases by one, to a minimum of one. You have disadvantage on all attack rolls while in this stance. You can switch to Dragon Stance without succeeding a melee attack as a minor action. Instead of Draconic Riposte, enemies who roll a hit in your Counter range now trigger 
    - **Vanguard Parry.** Instead of a dodge and a counterattack, you parry the enemy's attack as a reaction, throwing them off their balance and leaving them vulnerable. Your next melee attack against the creature has a +10 bonus to the roll. You may switch your stance.
