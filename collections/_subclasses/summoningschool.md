***Summoning is the art of conjuring creatures and objects to aid you in your endeavors. Wizards of the School of Summoning are often characterized by having many “helpers” around them at all times, even if they are not expecting danger. They are often joked about needing a summon to do everything for them, from eating to walking. However, most do not dare to say it to their faces, lest a flock of hawks descend upon them.***

| Wizard Level | Features |
|--------------|----------|
| 2nd          | Summoning Savant, Dedicated Summon |
| 6th          | Brutal Summon |
| 10th         | Devoted Summon |
| 14th         | Dual Destinies |

### Summoning Savant
Beginning when you select this school at 2nd level, the gold and time you must spend to copy a Conjuration spell that summons a creature with its own initiative is halved. Also, you may add these spells into your spellbook as if it were a wizard spell if it is not in the wizard spell list.

### Dedicated Summon
Starting at the 2nd level, when you choose this school, you may enchant a summon with a special bond to make them more effective. A summon is a creature conjured by a spell that has its own initiative. As an action, choose a creature you have summoned that you can see within 30 ft. While bonded, the following benefits are granted:
- If the spell used to summon the creature has a duration less than 24 hours, it remains for 24 hours or until it is otherwise dispelled.
- The summon is treated as a familiar as if it were summoned by the Find Familiar spell.
- The creature may take the Attack action, even if the spell does not allow it.
- When you take damage, you may use your reaction to transfer half of the damage taken to your summon while it is within 30 ft. of you.

Once bonded, you cannot use this feature again until the end of a long rest. You gain an extra use of Dedicated Summon at 6th, 10th, and 14th level.

### Brutal Summon
Beginning at the 6th level, when your Dedicated Summon takes the Attack action, it may add your Intelligence modifier to its damage rolls.

### Devoted Summon
Beginning at the 10th level, the bond with your Dedicated Summon reaches complete affinity, granting the following benefits:
- When your Dedicated Summon makes an ability check, saving throw, or attack, you may add your Intelligence modifier to the roll.
- As an action, you may expend a spell slot to cause your Dedicated Summon to regain a d6 of hit points for each level of spell slot expended.
- Your Dedicated Summon has advantage on attacks against creatures that are within 5 ft. of you.
- Once per long rest, you may cast a spell with the point of origin being from your Dedicated Summon.

### Dual Destinies
Beginning at the 14th level, you can transfer any effects from yourself to your Dedicated Summon. As a reaction to receiving a negative effect, you may transfer the effect to your Dedicated Summon. This can be used a number of times equal to your Intelligence modifier per long rest.
