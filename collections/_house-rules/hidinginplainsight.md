title: Hiding in Plain Sight
type: combat
finished: false
---
When making a Dexterity (Stealth) check to hide, if the creature(s) you are attempting to hide from are able to clearly see you without any cover or concealment, you have a -10 deduction to your roll. If at any point you are Hidden and end your turn Hiding in Plain Sight, you must make another Dexterity (Stealth) check with the deduction. You can not Hide in Plain Sight while within 5 ft. of a creature that can see or hear you. Creatures have advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks made to find you while you are Hiding in Plain sight.
