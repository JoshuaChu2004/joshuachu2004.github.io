In general, martial (non-magical) classes like the Fighter, Monk, or Barbarian tend to fall behind at mid-high levels. This disparity is usually caused by the intense utility that a spellcaster has coupled with the fact that most DMs (and I) do not throw multiple combats at the players in a single day, allowing spellcasters to Long Rest and replenish their precious Spell Slots more often. These techniques are abilities that help boost martial classes to be more in line with spellcasters.

Every 3rd level, Martial characters (every 6th level for half-casters such as Paladin and ranger) may choose a Focus technique from the list found here Phoenix's Book of Bullshit. A focus technique may only be chosen once and you can only take a focus technique from a class' list if you have levels in that class.
