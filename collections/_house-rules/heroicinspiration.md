You can be given Heroic Inspiration by the DM for doing something particularly interesting or in character, or by succeeding a Roll with Heroism. You can have a maximum of three Heroic Inspiration at once, though you lose all but one at the end of a session. If you have Heroic Inspiration, you can expend it any of the following ways:

- When making an ability check, saving throw, or attack roll, you may choose to reroll the die, taking the higher result. This must be done before knowing the outcome of the roll.
- When an ally you can see within 15 ft. fails a saving throw, you may choose to reroll their die as a reaction, taking the higher result. An ally may only benefit from this once per saving throw.
- When you start your turn and are Incapacitated, you may choose to take an action but gain a level of exhaustion.
