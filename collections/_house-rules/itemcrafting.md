**Requirements:** To make most items, you must have proficiency in an artisan's tools. You must also have the required item supplies to craft an item. Each rarity of item has a different cost, shown as a GP amount worth of supplies required. At most magic shops or sometimes others like herbalists, jewelers, and smiths, you can purchase item supplies directly with GP. Likewise, you may also forage in certain places to acquire supplies. Each item rarity has an established DC, which you will roll against throughout the brewing.

<div class="break"></div>

**Process:** You must have an actual artisan's tools to begin crafting an item, with the required supply cost immediately on hand. Once the crafting has started, the supplies are consumed and you must make a Arcana check against the rarity's DC. If someone else with proficiency in Arcana or the same artisan's tools is helping you, you may roll with advantage. For certain items, you may need to make a different skill check than Arcana. Once begun, you will have to roll this same check several more times before the crafting has completed. When the item is completed, if you rolled more failures than successes, then the item becomes unusable.

<div class="break"></div>

**Time:** The crafting progress can be paused and resumed at a later time. Each rarity specifies the minimum amount of time it takes before the item can be completed. It also specifies how many separate work periods you must spend to craft the item and how long each work period must be for the interaction to count. For each work period, you must make another check against the rarity's DC. You may only perform one work period per day.

- **Common:** 3 Days (3, 1 hr)
- **Uncommon:** 1 Week (7, 2 hrs)
- **Rare:** 3 Weeks (21, 3 hrs)
- **Very Rare:** 3 Months (24, 4 hrs)
- **Legendary:** 1 Year (36, 6 hrs)

**Cost:** Every item requires some amount of supplies, with its rarity corresponding to the cost.

- **Common:** 15 GP
- **Uncommon:** 40 GP
- **Rare:** 100 GP
- **Very Rare:** 450 GP
- **Legendary:** 1500 GP

**DC:** Every item has a DC that you must roll a Arcana check (with Alchemist Supplies), adding your proficiency to the roll (doubled if expertise), against.

- **Common:** DC 13
- **Uncommon:** DC 15
- **Rare:** DC 18
- **Very Rare:** DC 20
- **Legendary:** DC 25

**Learning how to make a item:** Every item has some amount of studying that needs to be done in order to create it, even if you are copying instructions from a written source. The amount of time is doubled if you are researching the item rather than receiving instructions.

- **Common:** 6 hours
- **Uncommon:** 12 hours
- **Rare:** 24 hours
- **Very Rare:** 72 hours
- **Legendary:** 192 hours
